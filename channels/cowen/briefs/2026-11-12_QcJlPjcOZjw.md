## TL;DR
Cowen says Bitcoin (~72K) is following the Q4 pattern he expected, as a false breakout above the May high gives way to a decline. He expects weakness for a few more weeks, at least to the December Fed meeting. A lower low would set up a stronger 2027 bull market. A higher low would still be short-term bullish, but he thinks BTC eventually tests the realized price (~54K) later in the cycle.

## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin is around $72K and is clearing previous major support areas. Each break gets a small bounce (21-week EMA, 20-week SMA, then the 200-day with a wick below). He reads the bounces as apathetic and weak.
2. He sees déjà vu with the early-2026 pattern of higher lows in January–March followed by giving up. He says the place the price may collapse from is the same place it collapsed from then.
3. He rejects the 2019 and 2023 analogies that bulls used after the break above the previous high. He argues it is a midterm year, so it looks more like a false breakout, as in 2019, 2015, 2020 and March 2022. Those turned difficult afterward.
4. March 2022 and now are similar in the dynamics after the first rate hike of a cycle: a rally, fading, then a fall. He adds that this cycle's low could come in Q4 or earlier. He admits he missed checking how BTC behaves after a September midterm-year hike: it holds about 1.5 months, then drops to the Q4 low (2018, 2022).
5. A higher low is possible, but it is not his main scenario. He says it would be more bearish long-term. His worry is repeated up-and-down chop, with IPOs such as SpaceX, Anthropic and possibly OpenAI draining Bitcoin as people sell it to buy the stock. He compares this to silver's multi-leg range before it broke out.
6. In 2018 BTC was already at 6K when stocks began to fall. This time BTC was at 80K when stocks started falling, so there is more room to fall. He expects weakness for a few more weeks, at least to the December Fed meeting and likely a bit beyond, but does not know the exact level.
7. Historically BTC lows have been below the realized price (~54K): 2014, 2018, 2020 and 2022. He asks why this time would differ.
8. A much lower low would reset on-chain indicators and likely give a really good bull market in 2027, because enough bears would turn bullish. A higher low could also produce a 2027 bull market, but he still expects a test of the realized price later in the cycle, probably alongside a recession. This would be like 2018 without the final collapse.
9. He says a higher low would be a short-term bullish signal for three to six months. He thinks many bears turn bullish by year-end.
10. He bought Bitcoin in July as he said he would. His one mistake was not believing the May high would be broken. He says bear markets make fools of both bulls and bears.

## Predictions made
- BTC stays weak for a few more weeks, at least until the December Fed meeting and probably a bit after.
- Many bears turn bullish by the end of the year, and BTC rises again in 2027.
- A lower low leads to a really good bull market in 2027.
- A higher low is bullish for roughly three to six months.
- BTC eventually reaches the realized price (~54K) later in the cycle, probably with a recession.
