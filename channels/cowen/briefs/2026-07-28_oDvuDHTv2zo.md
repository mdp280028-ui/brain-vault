## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Cowen says the recent rally over the last few weeks is a typical pattern for July of midterm years.
2. Monthly Bitcoin returns for July in past midterm years: 2022 was up 20%, 2018 was up almost 40%; this year Bitcoin is up about 10% so far.
3. In both 2022 and 2018, and also in 2014, the following August and September were red months.
4. He views 2018 as the best comparison for current market structure, though not necessarily for understanding liquidity and flows (where 2019 is a better analog).
