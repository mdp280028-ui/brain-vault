## TL;DR
Bitcoin's weekly RSI crashed back below the 900-day "white line" (~55) it reclaimed in August 2025, after a stochastic RSI cross down in early October near $87,000. He says momentum and price keep falling while the orange line is on top, and a weekly RSI drop below 33 would break the trend built since February.

## Main points
1. On the BTC/USD weekly RSI, a white line (~55) acted as resistance for 900 days. Bitcoin finally broke above it in August 2025 (RSI rallied to ~59), retested it as support in early September, then pushed to an RSI of ~63.5-64. He read this as strength, but the RSI has now fallen back through the line after holding it only a couple of months.
2. Weekly stochastic RSI is his timing tool. Crosses happen about twice a year. The May 2026 cross down took price from $81,000 to $57,000. The latest cross down came in early October near $87,000, and it dragged RSI below the white line. Orange line on top means momentum and price are falling; blue on top means rising.
3. Cross ups are his buy zones. The last was around $64,000-65,000, the one before it in February around $66,000-68,000. He doesn't expect another cross up this year but allows one at the very end of the year. He warns of possible fake-outs where blue crosses back up.
4. Structure to watch: since the March low, the weekly RSI has made two higher highs and one higher low. He wants a third higher high and a second higher low. The RSI can keep dropping while orange is on top, but must not fall below the 33 pivot low. A break below 33 would end the trend building since February and mean "real turmoil". A higher low above 33 would need to be followed by a higher high.
5. Two conditions to turn bullish: Bitcoin must battle back above the white line and hold it as support, while the stochastic RSI turns up.
