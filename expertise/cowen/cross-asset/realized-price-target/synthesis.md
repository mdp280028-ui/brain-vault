## Current Thesis
Cowen treats the realized price (~$53K, down from ~$54K a week earlier) as a level Bitcoin has historically fallen below in bear markets (2014–15, 2018, 2022), so a further drop to or below it remains possible. He rejects the claim that it no longer matters, and he argues against calling a bottom. Odds of a low have risen given the lower low in Q4 of a midterm year, but historical precedent (the realized price, post-rate-hike behavior, and Bitcoin bottoming after metals) suggests the window of weakness may stay open, with a bottom more credible in 2027 if no new low is made by then. This extends his earlier view that the bear market was close to ending in time, with a possible low around the mid-December FOMC meeting, not necessarily at the realized price.
