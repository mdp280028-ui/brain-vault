## TL;DR
The S&P 500 topped at 5,800 going into the election, per a recurring September/October pre-election topping pattern and a bearish volume-divergence structure matching 2022 and 2000/2008 — but he's not selling any meme coins, betting Bitcoin holds up like it has in every prior pre-election period.

## Main points
1. Every pre-election cycle he charted (2020, 2016, 2012, 2008, 2004, 2000) shows the S&P 500 topping and going nowhere or dropping in September/October, then rallying "up only" starting in November — except 2000 and 2008, when the pre-election top unwound into a multi-year bear market (dot-com bubble, financial crisis).
2. He calls the top "in" at SPX 5,800 (price was 5,756 and falling as he recorded), citing bearish price/volume divergence — price making higher highs while volume declines — plus a three-top-with-overshoot pattern he says is identical to the structure that preceded the 2022 top.
3. He can't know the duration of the top: recovery times after past pre-election tops ranged from ~30-33 days (fastest) to 2,300 days, 591-800 days, and 470+ days in the 2000/2008 cases. His base case is no new S&P all-time high until "late November at the earliest."
4. Speculates a Harris/Democrat win lines up more with the 2000/2008 "world falls apart" outcome, and a Trump win lines up more with the typical quick November recovery — but explicitly says either candidate could preside over a crash and he doesn't buy the pure "Democrats win = crash" narrative (notes 2020 rallied hard under Biden).
5. Key tell he's watching: in the last three election cycles (2020, 2016, 2012), Bitcoin was essentially unaffected by pre-election stock market volatility. If BTC/ETH hold up through this pre-election volatility, he'll read that as confirmation the bull market continues; if crypto sells off hard alongside stocks, that's a bad sign of broader market weakness.
6. Cites gold falling during the 2008 financial crisis as his reason to expect Bitcoin would also fall in a genuine financial-crisis-level scenario (not just a normal pre-election dip).
7. He is not selling any meme coins/crypto despite calling the stock top, because he sees 20x+ upside in meme coins if Bitcoin reaches $150K+, versus limited downside ("losing 50% will not" change his life) — he's positioned for the "up only after November" base case while hedging only a small short position (up ~$260K on it) against the tail risk.
8. Notes the dollar (DXY) has been strengthening off the range low even though most expected it to break down as the Fed cuts rates — calls this bearish for the broader "big bull market" thesis and says a real bull run resumes once the dollar breaks down.
