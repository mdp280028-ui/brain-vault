# Bull Market Support Band

A combined indicator (20-week SMA + 21-week EMA) Cowen treats as the bull/bear regime line for Bitcoin. Sustained closes below it signal bear-market continuation; reclaims signal regime shifts. Also applied to SPX and select altcoins.

## Current Thesis

BTC is pinned in a tightening squeeze between the falling bear market resistance band (~$69K) above and the rising 200-week MA (~$63.7K) below, now nearly converged, with Cowen arguing the two lines will force a decisive breakout or breakdown within the next couple of months and by year-end at the latest. He leans bearish into that resolution: seasonality (every prior midterm-year August has been negative) and an NVRV Z-score that hasn't fully reset both point to another leg down, likely back below $60K, before a cycle bottom forms around late November. For gold, the analogous monthly-timeframe band (20-month MA/21-month EMA) continues to play the opposite role — structural bull-market support — with Cowen reiterating that price has historically come back to test (and in the 2000s/1970s bull runs, even briefly dipped below) this band without ending the bull market; he still expects gold to carve its bottom in the June-to-October midterm-year window, pointing to a ~30% correction that's comparable to prior non-terminal bull-market drawdowns, before resuming higher into the pre-election year.

## Key Evidence

- The bear market resistance band (~$69K) is trending down while the 200-week MA (~$63.7K) is trending up, and BTC is now trading almost exactly at the 200-week MA (63.5K close vs. 63,551 average), squeezed between the two converging lines (WRftdLFrepU)
- In 2022, BTC bounced off the 200-week MA, had a green week in early August, then suffered a larger drawdown; in 2018 the drop began right as August started — both offered as analogues for the current squeeze (WRftdLFrepU)
- BTC posted its first weekly close below the 200-week MA this cycle in late June 2026, mirroring June 2022 and June 2018; the band now sits as overhead resistance above both the 200-week MA and spot price (prior)
- Gold's bull market support band (20-month SMA/21-month EMA) has historically been retested during bull markets, and even briefly broken below during the 2000s and 1970s bull runs, without marking the end of the bull market (mo9PUWbEJ-4)
- Gold's 2026 year-to-date ROI tracks closely with 2022 and 2018, both of which saw relief around this point in the year before one more period of later weakness; averaging all prior midterm years, gold tends to bottom in early July, with the broader window spanning June through October (mo9PUWbEJ-4)
- Gold's ~30% correction from highs is comparable to 20-30% drawdowns seen within prior gold bull markets that did not mark the end of the bull run (mo9PUWbEJ-4)
