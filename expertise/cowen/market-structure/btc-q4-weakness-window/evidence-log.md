# Evidence log: Btc Q4 Weakness Window

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-11-12 (QcJlPjcOZjw): Cowen says BTC (~72K) is following the Q4 pattern he expected, with a false breakout above the May high followed by a decline through key support areas that only get weak bounces. He compares it to midterm-year analogs (2015, 2019, 2020, March 2022) and to the early-2026 higher-lows-then-collapse pattern. He expects weakness to last until at least the December Fed meeting, and says a much lower low would set up a strong 2027 bull market. "The window of weakness is still open and we will likely continue to fall for a few more weeks." [10:37]
