## TL;DR
Kevin expects Nvidia to get through the chip-smuggling scandal and congressional testimony by pausing Southeast Asia shipments. He expects a Commerce Secretary, Lutnik, to take the blame in the end. He gives no price call on the stock.

## Main points
1. Bloomberg reports GB300 racks, sold by Gigabyte, reached a China site. The racks were used as loan collateral, so he thinks the whole stack may have known. He cites the DOJ case against a Super Micro co-founder, with hair dryers used to swap labels and dummy servers used to fool inspectors. DOJ alleges $500M of servers, but other reporting puts it at tens of billions.
2. Nvidia told Dell, Super Micro and others to halt shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia. He reads this as a political move ahead of congressional testimony, a self-inflicted "shooting ourselves in the foot" to show compliance. Nvidia also redefined customer location as the customer's headquarters instead of where chips end up. He says that makes revenue by country less informative. He cites US about $73B, Taiwan about $30B, China including Hong Kong $11B, and other $1.6B. China is forecast to fall to zero.
3. Jensen will not plead the Fifth, he thinks. Trump has said he doesn't have to, and he says Trump is willing to pardon him or take a government stake in Nvidia. He notes Nvidia's sales chief dated a Super Micro executive for years, and calls this ordinary business-people dealings rather than a criminal ring.
4. His forecast is that investigations will be framed as "everybody's a smuggler", and someone must take the fall. He picks Commerce Secretary Lutnik. Prediction markets give 63% odds he is out by January 2028, and 49% before July. Volume is $642K, and odds have slipped.
