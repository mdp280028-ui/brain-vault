# Logarithmic Regression Bands

Log-regression bands fitted to Bitcoin's all-time price history that Cowen uses to define long-term support, midline, and resistance trajectories. He treats deviation from the bands as a long-horizon valuation signal and a complement to the risk metric.

## Current Thesis

Cowen uses logarithmic regression bands to frame long-term valuation across a lower support band, a fair-value midline, and an upper bear market resistance band, applying the framework to both ETH and BTC. For ETH, having been rejected at the resistance band in May 2026, he expects it to stall at or below that band for the rest of the year and not durably break above it until 2027, echoing the 2022 rejection that persisted into the following January; his near-term target is the lower band / April 2025 low area (~$2,000-2,100). For BTC, he now describes price as "ping-ponging" between the bear market resistance band (upside) and the 200-week moving average (downside), with each successive test pushing further through the bands and the range visibly tightening — a companion, price-action framing of the same two-band structure. He expects a brief BTC rally over the next 2-4 weeks to fade by August-September, followed by a probable breakdown toward/through the lower band area (retest of the ~57k February low) later in the year as part of a market-cycle-bottom formation, alongside a 10-20% stock market correction.

## Key Evidence

- ETH rejected at the bear market resistance band (upper band) in May 2026, confirming the band as meaningful overhead resistance in the current cycle (prior)
- The lower regression band aligns precisely with the April 2025 lows, providing a concrete ETH price target for the anticipated decline; a ~40% drop from a July 2026 high would likewise put ETH around/slightly below that same area (~$2,000-2,100) (prior; OZAQDVkVuG8)
- In 2021, Cowen predicted the regression model's fair value for ETH would not cross $2,000 until 2026; ETH is now trading near that midline level, validating the model's long-range accuracy (prior)
- Cowen expects ETH to stall at or before the bear market resistance band and not durably break above it until 2027, citing the 2022 precedent where rejection persisted until January of the following year (OZAQDVkVuG8)
- BTC is "ping-ponging" between the bear market resistance band and the 200-week moving average, with each successive test breaching further through each band, indicating a tightening range (JY_wY8XXjYU)
- Cowen expects a retest of the ~57k February low (near/below the lower band zone) and possibly a slightly lower move later in 2026 to fully reset on-chain indicators (JY_wY8XXjYU)
