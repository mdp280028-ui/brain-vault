## TL;DR
Cowen says the market is exhausting because everyone waits for a headline, trades on it, and then it turns out to be false and the market rolls back. He says this has happened many times over the past year and again just a few days ago.

## Key points (the distilled video)
1. The market feels exhausting because participants seem to be waiting for some kind of headline.
2. A headline appears, and within a few hours it turns out not to be true.
3. This has happened many, many times over the last year: people see a headline, think one thing, then realize hours later it wasn't true.
4. Cowen thinks this depletes the markets to some extent, because everyone is trying to trade on the news and it then turns out to be false.
5. After the news is shown to be false, the market just rolls back.
6. It happened again just a few days ago, when some headlines appeared and were then almost debunked.
