## TL;DR
Live breaking-news stream on a nationwide FAA ground stop (cause unknown, officially a telecom/radio outage, not cyber), set against Iran tension. His only market call is an after-hours read: Nasdaq 100 down about 75bp, Hertz up, airlines down.

## Main points
1. The FAA ordered a nationwide ground stop of all departures after radio frequency outages at air route traffic control centers. Kevin, a former pilot, says he has never seen one. He counts three in 25 years: 9/11, the Jan 11, 2023 NOTAM outage (1h37m) and this one.
2. Duration: first notices said about an hour, then extended. Reports point to about 7 hours, to roughly 11pm ET (8pm Pacific). Denver was told 2100 local.
3. Scale: about 7,884 flights delayed and 348 cancelled at one point, later 9,000+ delayed and 600+ cancelled. Euro control suspended Europe-to-US departures, and the FAA asked Mexico to ground US-bound aircraft. Alaska and Hawaii flights were reportedly unaffected. It hits two weeks before Thanksgiving, with 2.7 million passengers a day expected.
4. Official line: DHS Secretary Mullen said it is a telecom outage, there is no sign of a nefarious attack, all flights are accounted for, and nothing is ruled out. He saw no link to Thursday's Microsoft outage. Kevin calls him "clueless". The FBI reportedly does not believe it was a cyber attack or criminality, and a software update glitch is one line of inquiry.
5. Possible causes: the WSJ says the outage appears to affect a telecom network overseen by L3Harris, which handles ATC radio frequencies. He stresses this is unverified and calls it "not great for L3 if true". An FAA modernization push (copper to fiber, analog to VoIP, 44% of surface awareness initiatives replaced) may be a factor, but he says that is not established. The FAA's AI routing tool rollout is reported as unrelated.
6. Geopolitical backdrop: air raid sirens at UK bases (Lakenheath) were blamed on an unauthorized drone and resolved. About 8 US aircraft were moved out of eastern England over 72 hours. Bessent was reportedly moved to a military base after a photo of his home was published.
7. Iran: Mossad's director is in Washington meeting the CIA director and Witkoff, as Netanyahu presses Trump to resume the war. Trump says he might strike "right now" if things go the other way. Trump is siding with the Gulf states on blockade and waiting, as Saudi Arabia says "Iran is disintegrating". Kevin says there is no progress toward a deal, which he calls "devastating".
8. Other items: Starship Flight 15 launched, with the booster diverting offshore (no catch). The National Weather Service radar outage was cleared. A report says institutional interest in the Anthropic IPO outpaces the early SpaceX road show.
9. Market reaction: Nasdaq 100 down about 75bp after hours, with the index near the 675 line he says everyone is watching. Hertz was up 10.6% and Delta down a couple of percent. Kevin says rental car stocks are being bought and airlines sold, but the root cause is unclear. The rest of the stream is course promotion, which is skipped here.
