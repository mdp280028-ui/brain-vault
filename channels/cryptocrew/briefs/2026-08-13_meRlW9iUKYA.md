## TL;DR
Ethereum has broken a 6-year trend line on its 4-month chart for the first time, a move he ties to a double-top reversal, and he expects both ETH and the broader altcoin market to struggle for years, with no real altcoin season until a possible reset around 2029-2031.

## Main points
1. On the ETH/USD 4-month Coinbase chart, a white trend line connecting support touches from January 2020, May 2020, May 2022 (long wick), and September 2022 has held for 6 years — and has now broken to the downside for the first time.
2. He attributes the break to a double top: ETH rallied from the 2022 bottom near $5,000, pulled back to confirm support around the "roughly 4,000" area, held a long lower wick in January 2025 signaling buying pressure, rallied again into the ~$5,000 area, then formed a second top near the same price as the prior cycle top — a pattern he calls a classic trend-reversal signal, with upside wicks marking heavy profit-taking that built downward momentum ("like a rock tumbling down a mountain").
3. He identifies two lower price channels below the broken trend line: one from roughly $2,200 down to $1,400, and a second from $1,400 down to roughly $870 (an area tied to 2017 resistance that became 2022 support).
4. He says many "OG altcoins" show similar double-top patterns, and since altcoins had their glory days mainly in 2017 and have struggled since, he thinks this ETH trend-line break marks a broader reset point where altcoins "find their true identity."
5. Forecast: he does not think ETH is dead but expects it and altcoins broadly to struggle; he predicts people will keep waiting for an altcoin season through 2028-2029 without it materializing, the market turns down starting in 2030, and "reality sets in" by 2031 that altcoins may never be the same — though he says they will eventually regain power similar to his Bitcoin cycle thesis.
