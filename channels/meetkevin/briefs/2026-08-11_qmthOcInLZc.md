## TL;DR
Kevin argues a buried clause in Elon's Tesla CEO pay package lets all operational milestones (FSD subscriptions, deliveries, robotaxis, Optimus) get waived in a Tesla-SpaceX merger, meaning Elon could unlock huge stock comp tranches purely off a premium acquisition price — good for Tesla shareholders getting a buyout premium, but a risk for SpaceX shareholders if SpaceX overpays.

## Main points
1. Kevin says a clause on page 4-5 of Tesla's 2025 CEO Performance Award Agreement states that "notwithstanding" the operational milestones (10M FSD subs, 20M cumulative vehicle deliveries, 1M robotaxis, 1M Optimus deliveries, EBITDA tiers), in the event of a "change in control" (i.e., a SpaceX-Tesla merger) all operational milestones are disregarded and only the market-cap tranche matters, valued at the share price immediately before the acquisition.
2. He cites current progress against those milestones as evidence they're far from being met organically: 1.48M FSD subscriptions (only ~700K paying monthly) vs. 10M target (~14% there); ~9.7M cumulative vehicle deliveries vs. 20M target (~halfway); 20 unsupervised robotaxis vs. 1M target; 0 commercial Optimus units vs. 1M target.
3. Citing a Wall Street Journal article ("See how a Tesla SpaceX merger gives Musk a shortcut to his trillion-dollar payday"), Kevin walks through a scenario where SpaceX acquires Tesla at a $2 trillion valuation (~$506/share, a 53% premium to current price), which would let Elon unlock his first stock-comp tranche immediately regardless of milestones, netting him ~$17.9B pre-tax in stock options — plus his existing 12.7% Tesla stake would be worth ~$250B at that valuation, which converts into SpaceX stock upon merger.
4. Kevin's take: this is "brilliant... though kind of evil" optionality by Elon since it's not what shareholders thought they voted for, but Tesla shareholders likely won't mind getting bought out at a large premium; the real risk falls on SpaceX shareholders (he notes he's one) if SpaceX overpays for Tesla without the operational milestones actually being earned, since that dilutes/disadvantages SpaceX ownership even as it inflates Elon's stock comp and Tesla-stake value.
