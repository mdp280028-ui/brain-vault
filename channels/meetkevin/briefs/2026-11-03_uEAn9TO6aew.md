## TL;DR
Kevin sees a US–Iran memorandum of understanding as the likely catalyst to end the selloff, with oil down from 117 to 102. He calls the market oversold at a 13x forward PE and expects a post-election rally, provided the jobs data holds up.

## Main points
1. Iran: reports say the US approved a draft memorandum of understanding from Qatari mediators. Iran reportedly made concessions on the nuclear file and frozen assets. He says it is "a signature and a truth social away" and hopes it lands today. Trump also said Israeli aid ends at the end of the year.
2. Oil: it is down about 2.4% on the day, and from 117 to 102 over 5 days. It fell 7% yesterday. He jokes that Trump's advisors are shorting oil.
3. Selloff: yesterday was the first -1.5 standard deviation day on the Nasdaq 100 in 250 trading days. The prior ones were Jan 21, 2022 and a dot-com-era day in 2001. QQQ lost 700 two days in a row and is rejecting 700.
4. Valuation: the average forward PE is about 13, versus Cisco's 10-20 after the dot-com crash. He calls it cheap and an opportunity, but says technically the market hasn't crashed yet. Goldman's memory basket fell 22% in October and semis fell 18.6%. Optical names (Marvell, Lumentum-type "Marll and Light") fell 29%.
5. Leverage and memory: leveraged ETFs had to dump $1.9B of Micron in one day. He says not to be in leverage now. Micron trades at a 0.33 PEG, and he argues it should be ~5x higher even after a cyclicality discount. Samsung is down 35% from its late-May peak.
6. Memory fundamentals: Goldman's September data shows chips up 151% year over year, prices up 128%, DRAM up 368% and NAND up 510%. SK Hynix has 14 long-term deals, with demand exceeding supply through 2030. CXMT margins are ~89.5% with revenue up 613%, and Bernstein sees it passing Micron in wafer capacity by late 2028. GPU rental rates keep rising: B300 rentals are up 106% since July, while token prices are down 58%.
7. Labor data: ADP tomorrow is expected at 66K (prior 90K), and BLS Friday at 65K versus 29K last. A miss is the "real red flag" for recession. Goldman attributes 207K layoffs to AI since 2023. Only 49% of laid-off workers found equal or better pay, versus 62% two years ago. The household survey added 46K jobs in September.
8. Election and Fed: Kalshi gives Democrats a 77% chance of taking the Senate. He cites the stock market rising 100% of the time in the 6 months after elections since 1950, by 13% on average. Kalshi gives a 29% chance of "one and done" hikes, and Treasury refunding is Wednesday.
9. Positioning: tech positioning is at the 40th percentile, implying 10% EPS growth versus 47% printed in Q3. The last five rotations back into tech averaged a 38% gain. Deutsche Bank sees no capitulation.
10. Company points: Intel fell 29% despite ASPs up 54%. Meta is not expected to be cash-flow positive until 2028. Bill Ackman says SpaceX may need to borrow $1T, which Kevin had flagged a week ago.
11. Breadth: the S&P 500 fell on a day when 80%+ of its stocks rose, for the first time in 40 years. RSP (equal-weight S&P) is basically at all-time highs, so he says the market is "doing totally fine" overall.
