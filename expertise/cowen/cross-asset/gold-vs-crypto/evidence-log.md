He holds gold and expects continued strength: "Gold does the best under Republican sweep, which is exactly where we are right now." [25:03] Bitcoin's midterm-year historical average of −65% further widens the near-term performance gap between the two assets, weakening the "digital gold" equivalence thesis on a regime-adjusted basis.
