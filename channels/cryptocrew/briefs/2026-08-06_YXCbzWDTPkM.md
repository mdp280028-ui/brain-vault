## TL;DR
A 13-year-old trend line on Bitcoin's 6-month chart that has always acted as support just broke to the downside for the first time, which he reads as the start of a multi-cycle "bloodbath" where BTC may never return above that line again.

## Main points
1. On the BTC/USD 6-month candle chart, a trend line drawn from January 2011 acted as resistance through 2011–2013, then flipped to support and held through the 2015 bottom, the 2020 COVID crash, the 2022 bottom (~15-17K), the 2023 bull run start, and again in July 2025 — 13 years of consistent support.
2. Each of the last three cycle tops (2013, 2017, 2021) rallied to roughly 90%+ above this trend line before topping, measured from the trend line up to the top of the candle; he calls this a sign of a "healthy market."
3. In the most recently completed cycle, price only rallied to about half that historical percentage above the trend line before topping — the first time the pattern of ~90% extensions broke.
4. The most recent 6-month candle closed below the trend line, breaking 13 years of support into resistance for the first time; he argues each cycle's percentage gain from bear-market bottom to bull-market top has been shrinking cycle over cycle, and this break confirms that deceleration trend.
5. Forecast: the next market cycle top (end of 2029/beginning of 2030) is likely to only reach back up to this now-resistance trend line — meaning BTC may never trade above it again — implying a "devastation period" lasting multiple cycles before a healthy 15-year cycle structure could eventually repeat.
