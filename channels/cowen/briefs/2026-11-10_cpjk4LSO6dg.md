## TL;DR
In a 5.5-hour live preview of his upcoming conference, Cowen and a string of guests debated whether Bitcoin's Q4 midterm-year low is in. Cowen still expects further weakness tied to stocks, with BTC at about 73K. He says a low in the next two months, either higher or lower than the prior low, is the window. After that he sees a 2027 rally, possibly with a weak back half.

## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin is around 73K, near the 200-day moving average, after being at 81K on Nov 4 and at 87K in the recent high. Cowen says the stock market drives the Q4 weakness. The Nasdaq is down about 15%, the S&P is only about 6% down, and BTC could drop further if stocks make new lows. In 2014, 2018 and 2022 every Q4 low came with a stock drop.
2. He frames the 87K high as a fake-out above the prior high. The 2026 top was apathetic, with social interest matching 2019. Apathetic tops are more prone to fake-outs in both directions, and a higher high does not prove a bull market. He cites silver 2011, Bitcoin 2015 and 2019 as precedents.
3. Realized price (~54K) and balance price (~38K) have not been breached this bear market, but every prior bear market breached them. He gives two scenarios. In one, a higher low around the low 60s leads to a weaker 2027 rally and a later drop to the realized price once the stock bear market arrives. In the other, a 2018-style lower low in Q4 resets the on-chain indicators, which he would find more bullish.
4. His timing window is the next two months, roughly to mid-January. If no new low comes by then, he expects none for about nine or ten months. He expects weakness at least into the December FOMC, with a possible bottom around it. He compares this to 2018, when a September hike, a skipped meeting and a December hike were followed by a Bitcoin bottom and the projected 2019 hikes never came.
5. Bitcoin has fallen ahead of the Anthropic IPO, as it did before the SpaceX IPO, which suggests liquidity is being pulled away. Anthropic cut its target valuation from 2T to 1.5T to 1T. His own IPO approach is to ignore the first month or two and buy after the surge fades.
6. Matt Crosby is bullish. Bitcoin's year-over-year return vs global M2 shows a record divergence that typically appears at the tail end of bear markets. His base case is new all-time highs in 2027. He argues the median realized price and an amplitude-decay model support a bottom near where Bitcoin bottomed.
7. Alessio Rossani is bullish on stocks and Bitcoin as long as support holds. His line in the sand for BTC is 69–70K, and he is watching for a Dow theory sell signal. He expects a top in 2027 and a bigger drop in metals next year.
8. Cowen thinks metals may be bottoming and says they have usually bottomed before risk assets. He would give up the bullish metals view if gold is not near highs by mid-2027. He thinks the Fed is near the end of its hiking cycle (one more hike, maybe two). He thinks the 10-year yield is near a top but will be higher in 10 years, and he has bought TIPS and some TLT.
9. Mike Novogratz-style bearish guests: Mike McGlone is bearish on everything except bonds, crude and gold, and says the Fed is priced for hikes. Gareth Soloway thinks Bitcoin has bottomed but could retrace to about 67K. Evan is in dip-buying mode, expecting 69K or the mid-60s, then 90K in Q1 2027. FA is accumulating down to 60.8K.
10. A crisis or a stock capitulation would probably push Bitcoin down first, then lead to looser policy that favors it. Cowen says AI is arguably holding Bitcoin back because AI capex keeps policy tight, and he suggests bears and bulls can both be right depending on timing.

## Predictions made
- BTC finds its Q4 low (higher or lower) within about two months, by roughly mid-January 2027. If not by then, probably not for nine or ten months.
- BTC weak at least until the December FOMC, with a possible bottom around then.
- A BTC rally to 90–100K in early 2027 is possible, with a weak second half of 2027.
- Bitcoin likely to hit the realized price eventually, possibly not until a stock bear market in 2027–2028.
- Fed: one more hike, maybe two, and then the hiking cycle ends. The 10-year yield tops soon at a lower high. Cuts maybe late 2027.
- Metals bounce; Cowen drops the bullish gold view if gold is not near highs by mid-2027.
