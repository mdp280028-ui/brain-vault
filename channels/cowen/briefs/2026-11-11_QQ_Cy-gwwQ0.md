## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Cowen still expects weakness in Q4. His change is that the low could be higher rather than lower than the prior low. He says he was wrong about the May high, but he rejects the idea that he has capitulated to the bulls.
2. He still thinks Bitcoin will eventually fall to its realized price (~54K). The balance price is ~38K, which he says could be reached in a future recession. He does not know whether that happens this year.
3. Timing: a drop below 57K has to come in the next two months. If it does not, he thinks it probably will not come for at least 10 months. He expects many bears to turn bullish by year-end, and expects the bullish stretch to last probably through the first half of 2027.
4. He describes the price path as stair-stepping down: 83K, then 80K, then the 21-week EMA, then the 20-week, and now the 200-day. If the supports keep failing, BTC could drop 20% quickly into the low-to-upper 60Ks. That is where the choice between a higher macro low and a much bigger drop would be decided.
5. A lower low would make him more bullish if it resets on-chain indicators over 3–4 months, as in 2018 and early 2019. A fast bounce with no accumulation he would still read as weakness, like a "hope rebound" in XRP or other altcoins.
6. The 2018 analogy: the Fed was hawkish and expected to hike twice more in 2019, then did not hike, and Bitcoin rallied. Two hikes are now expected in 2027, and a falling Nasdaq could cancel them. A 2019-style rally from there could still be a macro lower high.
7. Guest Jason Pasino sees no macro confirmation of a stock-market peak yet, and notes housing developers declining. Cowen expects a pretty strong rebound by 2027 after this downturn, and thinks stocks could crash over the next two years. Gold peaked in January and has been weak, and Jason says silver is unlikely to reach a new high.
8. Cowen holds mostly stocks, and has recently bought bonds and inflation-protected Treasuries. He views bonds as attractive over a 2–3 year horizon. His long-term view is that the 10-year yield rises to about 6.5% over the next four years, and that rates may be in a multi-decade rising cycle, with a lost-decade risk for speculative assets.
9. Guy argues that AI-driven web traffic is breaking the ad-funded internet. He is building a payment gate where AI agents pay in USDC via x402. Both agree stablecoins and agent payments are crypto's best real-world use case, and a Paraguayan bank failure showed why self-custody assets like Bitcoin exist.
10. Cowen says crypto is culturally out of fashion and altcoins are fading. Even a rally to 150K probably would not bring people back, though the industry is better for the pain.

## Predictions made
- BTC breaks below 57K only if it happens within about two months; otherwise probably not for at least 10 months.
- BTC likely to reach the realized price (~54K) eventually, timing unknown.
- Bullish stretch lasting through the first half of 2027.
- If BTC rallies, a target range of 90K–150K.
- 10-year Treasury yield rises to about 6.5% over the next four years.
- Stock market crash likely over the next two years.
- Two Fed rate hikes expected in 2027, possibly canceled if the Nasdaq keeps falling.
