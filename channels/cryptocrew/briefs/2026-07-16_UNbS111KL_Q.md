## TL;DR
Using the 21/50 weekly MA cross as a cycle-timing tool, he says Bitcoin is repeating its 2015/2019/2022 post-top pattern almost exactly, with roughly 170-180 of a typical ~400-day bloodbath window elapsed — implying the next bull run starts late 2026 to Q1 2027.

## Main points
1. He overlays the weekly 21 MA (green) and 50 MA (yellow) on BTC/USD and shows that in 2015 (Aug 2014 cross), 2019 (July 2018 cross), and 2022 (March 2022 cross), the green-over-yellow cross always occurred after the cycle top, followed by a "gruesome pocket" (bloodbath) down to a support zone.
2. In each prior cycle, the wait from cross-down to cross-back-up (marking cycle bottom / start of new bull) was roughly 400 days (2015 ~400 days, 2019 ~400-ish days, 2022 ~400-ish days, sometimes as low as ~350).
3. Claims this exact setup has now recurred: the current cross happened after the recent market top (~$126,000, following a bull run from ~$25,000), and price has already fallen into a support zone, matching all three prior cycles.
4. States BTC is currently ~170-180 days past this cycle's cross, i.e., "a little bit less than 200 days" into the ~400-day window — meaning roughly halfway through the chaos period before the next bull run.
5. His only condition for changing his bearish/bottoming thesis is a swing high above $97,000; anything less he calls "1,000% irrelevant" and expects it to roll over like every prior bear market. He extrapolates the next bull run start to late 2026 or Q1 2027, and advises minimizing trading, buying only near the bottom window, and selling near the top window every few years rather than frequent trading.
