# Logarithmic Regression Bands

Log-regression bands fitted to Bitcoin's all-time price history that Cowen uses to define long-term support, midline, and resistance trajectories. He treats deviation from the bands as a long-horizon valuation signal and a complement to the risk metric.

## Current Thesis

Cowen uses logarithmic regression bands to frame long-term valuation across a lower support band, a fair-value midline, and an upper bear market resistance band, applying the framework to both ETH and BTC. For ETH, having been rejected at the resistance band in May 2026, he expects it to stall at or below that band for the rest of the year and not durably break above it until 2027, echoing the 2022 rejection that persisted into the following January; his near-term target is the lower band / April 2025 low area (~$2,000-2,100). For BTC, he continues to frame price action around the bear market resistance band as the key overhead level: the July rally (following the 2018 seasonal template of a February low, a higher low in spring, a swept low in late June/early July, then a rally into resistance) is expected to get rejected near that band in late July/early August, pull back into August-September, and possibly complete a final drop toward the lower band / cycle-bottom zone in Q4. He notes BTC historically hasn't durably broken above the bear market resistance band until well into the post-halving year (January, March, or June/October in prior cycles), reinforcing that any sustained breakout is still likely far off.

## Key Evidence

- ETH rejected at the bear market resistance band (upper band) in May 2026, confirming the band as meaningful overhead resistance in the current cycle (prior)
- The lower regression band aligns precisely with the April 2025 lows, providing a concrete ETH price target for the anticipated decline; a ~40% drop from a July 2026 high would likewise put ETH around/slightly below that same area (~$2,000-2,100) (prior; OZAQDVkVuG8)
- In 2021, Cowen predicted the regression model's fair value for ETH would not cross $2,000 until 2026; ETH is now trading near that midline level, validating the model's long-range accuracy (prior)
- BTC is "ping-ponging" between the bear market resistance band and the 200-week moving average, with each successive test breaching further through each band, indicating a tightening range (JY_wY8XXjYU)
- Historically, BTC hasn't durably broken above the bear market resistance band until well into the post-halving year — January, March, or June/October in prior cycles — implying a long wait before a durable breakout is typical (lfYYC7qxDQ8)
- 2018 is the preferred structural analog for the current approach to the resistance band: February low, higher low in spring, swept low in late June/early July, then rally into the band by late July/early August (lfYYC7qxDQ8)
