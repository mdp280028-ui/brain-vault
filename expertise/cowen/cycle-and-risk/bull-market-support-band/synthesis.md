# Bull Market Support Band

A combined indicator (20-week SMA + 21-week EMA) Cowen treats as the bull/bear regime line for Bitcoin. Sustained closes below it signal bear-market continuation; reclaims signal regime shifts. Also applied to SPX and select altcoins.

## Current Thesis

BTC is pinned in a tightening squeeze between the falling bear market resistance band (~$69K) above and the rising 200-week MA (~$63.7K) below, now nearly converged, with Cowen arguing the two lines will force a decisive breakout or breakdown within the next couple of months and by year-end at the latest. He leans bearish into that resolution: seasonality (every prior midterm-year August has been negative) and an NVRV Z-score that hasn't fully reset both point to another leg down, likely back below $60K, before a cycle bottom forms around late November. For gold, the analogous monthly-timeframe band (20-month MA/21-month EMA) continues to play the opposite role — structural bull-market support — pinpointed at ~$3,800, with Cowen still expecting a tag-and-bounce in the July-to-October window as gold's midterm-year correction completes, prioritizing timing the low over its exact depth.

## Key Evidence

- The bear market resistance band (~$69K) is trending down while the 200-week MA (~$63.7K) is trending up, and BTC is now trading almost exactly at the 200-week MA (63.5K close vs. 63,551 average), squeezed between the two converging lines (WRftdLFrepU)
- In 2022, BTC bounced off the 200-week MA, had a green week in early August, then suffered a larger drawdown; in 2018 the drop began right as August started — both offered as analogues for the current squeeze (WRftdLFrepU)
- In 2022 BTC never even reached the bear market resistance band before the August correction, and didn't tag it again until October, by which point the band had fallen well below August price levels — illustrating reclaim risk isn't guaranteed (WRftdLFrepU)
- Bitcoin is squeezed between the 200-week MA and the bear market resistance band, mirroring the 2018 fractal structure (Feb low, higher low in March/April, May rejection off the band, sweep of the Feb low in late June/early July) (prior)
- BTC posted its first weekly close below the 200-week MA this cycle in late June 2026, mirroring June 2022 and June 2018; the band now sits as overhead resistance above both the 200-week MA and spot price (prior)
- Gold's bull market support band sits at ~$3,800 (~4% below current price of ~$4,000); gold hasn't tagged this band since 2023, briefly broke below it in 2022 and 2008, with 2018/2022 midterm-year comps pointing to an average August–September bottom window (prior)
