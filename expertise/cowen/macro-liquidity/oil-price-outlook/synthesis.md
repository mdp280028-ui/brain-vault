# Oil Price Outlook

Crude oil price trajectory and its role as a financial-conditions input. Cowen tracks oil as a component of the macro backdrop alongside DXY and inflation, treating sustained oil strength as a tightening force on liquidity and a headwind for risk assets.

## Current Thesis

Oil remains structurally higher, and Cowen now expects a further leg up: Brent toward 118–120 with a possible new all-time high (above 135), driven by the Iran war and a stated attack after the midterms. The earlier mechanism still holds: Strait of Hormuz disruptions were masked by SPR drawdowns, but shortages and an inflation spike make rate hikes, not cuts, the likely Fed response under Warsh, and he now expects a hike around December alongside a rallying dollar. Elevated oil keeps financial conditions tight and reinforces the bearish Q4 midterm-year view for risk assets, with equities (Nasdaq, Google, Intel) already selling off.

## Key Evidence

- Brent is seen going higher, to about 118–120 with a possible new all-time high, because of the Iran war and a stated attack after the midterms (g2MQiby7_qY)
- Dollar expected to rally into December with a rate hike likely then, the macro combination that tightens liquidity alongside high oil (g2MQiby7_qY)
- Strait of Hormuz closure is the primary inflation driver; SPR drawdowns buffered the shock, but reserves are nearly exhausted, with shortages of oil, naphtha, and fertilizer imminent (8PQee-onXrU)
- Rate hike risk rises as shortage inflation spikes: "my guess is that inflation is going to spike because of these shortages really starting to bite because of reserves running out and the only option then is to raise rates" [20:21]; Warsh will not risk a "transitory" repeat (8PQee-onXrU)
- PPI spiking sharply (~6% MoM print cited) and CPI re-accelerating YoY, with oil a direct input to both; the 21-week EMA already supported oil once in 2026 (FgxAe_NAh5c)
- Energy stocks not yet at cycle top; historical pattern (2000, 2007) shows energy peaks 6–12 months after the equity market peak (8PQee-onXrU)
