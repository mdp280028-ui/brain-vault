# Evidence log: Bond Market Yield Curve

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-07-28 (TOKUXlPBhOs): Cowen frames the yield curve as the market's real inflation signal rather than a labor-market warning, pointing to strong jobless claims (187,000) and falling unemployment (4.2%) alongside a two-year yield that has climbed above the Fed funds rate. He expects the 10-year to retest its October 2023 high and the 30-year to finally break its 5.1-5.2% ceiling after making higher lows since 2023, which he says reflects market pricing of a 4.25% Fed funds rate by December. "The Fed tracks the two-year yield." [11:07]
