## Current Thesis

Cowen's 2018-fractal playbook remains on track: the late June/early July low and July countertrend rally both played out as called, and he now frames the market as entering the back half of the pattern — a summer "low" (not necessarily "the low") followed by drying volatility and a late-year "crunch" event that resolves the cycle bottom.
