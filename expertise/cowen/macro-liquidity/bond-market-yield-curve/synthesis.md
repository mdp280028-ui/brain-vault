## Current Thesis
Cowen argues the bond market, not the labor market, is the key macro risk: the long end (10-year, 30-year) is poised to break higher as "bond vigilantes" push yields up on inflation concerns, while the two-year yield trading above the Fed funds rate signals policy is no longer restrictive enough. He expects this dynamic to force the Fed to hold in July but hike in September, with the resulting yield pressure triggering a 10-20% stock correction in the August-September window and dragging Bitcoin down to its Q4 cycle bottom.
