# Evidence log: Spacex Ipo Valuation And Lockup

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-06-18 (xutthOFM_RA): The SpaceX IPO opened at ~$130 and ran to a high near $220 (~50% gain), but the first major insider unlock — releasing over 20% of the float — hits August 11th, and all three hosts expect selling pressure to follow. Guy frames the current 130x revenue valuation as "insane" and draws a historical parallel to Tesla's IPO, noting that ~90% of IPOs eventually trade below their IPO-day price and that SpaceX launched at a valuation "many orders of magnitude higher" than Tesla's was at its IPO. Despite the stretched valuation, Guy signals genuine long-term interest once the post-unlock dip materializes, possibly as early as August.

- 2026-10-30 (g2MQiby7_qY): The video barely touches this topic. While laying out his "bounce at the band, then a final drop after the midterms" scenario for Bitcoin, Cowen likens that bounce to the pre-SpaceX-IPO price action and says it is now being compared with the upcoming Anthropic IPO. He says nothing about SpaceX's valuation, the lockup, or post-IPO performance, so this adds no evidence on the thesis itself.

- 2026-11-10 (cpjk4LSO6dg): SpaceX appears only as a comparison for Anthropic. Cowen shows Bitcoin 30-day overlays against the SpaceX and Anthropic IPOs and says Bitcoin fell ahead of both, which suggests liquidity is being pulled away. He adds that his own IPO approach is to ignore the first month or two and buy after the surge fades, and that Anthropic cut its target valuation from 2T to 1.5T to 1T. The brief has no SpaceX valuation or lockup detail.
