## Key points (the distilled video)
1. The Anthropic IPO is priced at $83 a share, a fully diluted valuation of about $1.5T. He believes it is the second-largest IPO by market cap. SpaceX was about $1.75T at launch and about $2.1T after day one.
2. His usual IPO pattern is a surge on launch, then a drop of about 50% that tends to give a better entry. SpaceX surged from $149 to $225 (+50%) and then fell about 53%. The surge often runs further than people expect, pulls people in, and then dumps.
3. IPO outcomes after the drop vary. Tesla (2010) made a higher low and a macro low. Coinbase and Rivian went to lower lows. Apple fell about 40%, rallied, then made a lower low in the 1982 recession. He says the drop is usually the better time to buy than FOMOing in on launch.
4. Nasdaq futures are up about 2% on launch day (a Thursday). SpaceX launched on a Thursday with futures up about 3%. After that, markets rallied, then made a slightly lower low about 32 trading days later.
5. Bitcoin is near its SpaceX IPO-day level (~63.5–63.8K then, ~64K now) and made a new local low going into the IPO, as it did before SpaceX. After SpaceX, BTC bounced and then made a slightly lower low about 18–20 days later. That date lines up with the December 9 FOMC meeting.
6. Since July, 21st-of-the-month dates have marked local BTC highs (July, September and October). If BTC bounces, he says to watch November 21. He compares this to the late-November 2018 local top and a bottom in mid-December 2018, just after the FOMC meeting.
7. Another government shutdown is possible on December 12. BTC on average sold off, levelled out, and sold off again into past shutdowns, then rose afterward. In 2018 it fell, bounced, and sold off into the 4-year cycle low.
8. Bitcoin ETF flows show large outflows into the Anthropic IPO (about $306M out on Nov 18). He says liquidity leaves crypto around massive IPOs. During SpaceX, outflows stalled on IPO day, then resumed and coincided with a lower BTC low weeks later.
9. If BTC doesn't bounce, he would look to the 2018 path: a small rally, then a continued dump. Daily RSI is about 24. He warns against assuming oversold means a bounce, because in November 2018 RSI hit 17 and then 9 before the low.
10. If the Anthropic IPO plays out like SpaceX, he expects markets to cool around the December FOMC and then bounce into 2027. The OpenAI IPO is likely in 2027. He thinks the odds favor a surge on launch and a pullback within a couple of weeks.

## Predictions made
- Anthropic IPO: surge on launch, then a pullback within a couple of weeks.
- BTC local low around the December 9 FOMC meeting (about 18–20 days out), possibly alongside the Anthropic IPO coming back down.
- If BTC bounces, it tops near November 21.
- Markets cool around the December FOMC and bounce into 2027 if the SpaceX pattern repeats.
