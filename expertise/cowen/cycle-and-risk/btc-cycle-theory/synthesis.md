# BTC Cycle Theory

Cowen's framework that Bitcoin and broader risk assets follow an approximately four-year cycle of bull-top, bear-bottom, and accumulation phases. He frames the cycle as macro-driven rather than halving-driven, citing parallel equity-market cycle precedents going back to the 1950s.

## Current Thesis

Cowen's core call is unchanged but now has a firmer timing spine: July's rally is the expected midterm-year relief bounce (BTC +10.5% vs. +20% in 2022 and +38% in 2018), and he expects the "window of weakness" to reassert within 2-3 weeks, landing in August/September per the 2022/2018/2014 seasonal precedent, with a bond-market echo of 2023 (rising 10-year yield while the Fed holds) reinforcing the case. He's grown more explicit that no one can know for certain whether BTC even reaches the bear market resistance band or 200-day MA this cycle — in 2018 the rally faded just short of the 200-day MA, in 2022 neither level was reached until much later (Nov/Jan) — but he now attaches a concrete resolution window, using the ROI-from-low chart to place the cycle bottom within a few months, likely at or near November 2026. This marks a shift from pure range-bound ambiguity toward a dated bottom call, while he continues to recommend DCA over trying to time the exact low.

## Key Evidence

- July monthly-return comparison: BTC +10.5% this July (per 7vNA0geUryY) vs. +20% in July 2022 and ~38-40% in July 2018 — a muted rally consistent with, not contradicting, the seasonal pattern. (7vNA0geUryY, prior)
- In 2022, 2018, and 2014 alike, the July rally was followed by red August/September months (2014 saw all three months red), the core precedent for expecting renewed weakness in the next 2-3 weeks. (7vNA0geUryY, prior)
- New this brief: rising 10-year Treasury yield while the Fed holds is flagged as a bearish echo of 2023, when the same setup coincided with BTC turning down from July — bond market "starting to revolt" even without a rate hike. (7vNA0geUryY)
- Bitcoin has repeatedly failed to decisively clear the bear market resistance band or 200-day MA; Cowen stresses no one can know for certain if it reaches either this cycle, citing 2018 (close but no touch) and 2022 (reached only much later, Nov/Jan) as divergent precedents. (7vNA0geUryY, JY_wY8XXjYU)
- ROI-from-low day-count framework points to a cycle bottom within a few more months, with the market at or near a low by around November 2026. (7vNA0geUryY, oj2n8nkTTTE, sy6AxBbgico, XQN1M-sIuGs)
- Social participation (~0.25) matches July 2018 rather than July 2022 (~0.5); on-chain indicators remain only partially reset, with a retest of the ~57k February low seen as needed to complete the reset. (XQN1M-sIuGs, OZAQDVkVuG8, UIv9IQ4uXEA, prior)
