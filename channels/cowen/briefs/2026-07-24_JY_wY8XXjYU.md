## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin is oscillating between two key technical levels it can't durably break: the bear market resistance band (upside) and the 200-week moving average (downside).
2. Each successive test of these bands has gone further through them (fake-outs above the resistance band, fake-outs below the 200-week MA), showing the range is tightening.
3. This ping-ponging lines up with the four-year cycle's tendency to produce lows in late June/early July followed by a bounce in July, as seen in 2018, 2022, and now 2026.
4. Midterm-year July returns: 2026 up ~9% so far, 2022 up 20%, 2018 up almost 40%, 2014 slightly negative — July is typically less bad than the surrounding months.
5. In both 2022 and 2018, gains made in a green July were given back in August/September; Cowen expects the same pattern this cycle, with Bitcoin's strength window ending in roughly two to four weeks.
6. Baseline expectation: Bitcoin breaks down later in the year around the same time the stock market suffers a 10-20% drop (as in 2014, 2018, 2022), forming the market cycle bottom before the next bull market.
7. If Bitcoin does not break down by year-end, Cowen says he'd pivot to a time-based capitulation view and consider the bull market already resumed.
8. On-chain indicators are only partially reset; Cowen expects a retest of the February low (~57k) and possibly a slightly lower move later in the year to fully reset them.
9. Cowen reiterates his S&P call: a shallow drop in June, a bounce back up in July, then a top in August or September (matching S&P tops in the last three midterm years), followed by an S&P drop that drags Bitcoin down into the cycle bottom.
10. He likens 2026 Bitcoin to "a less volatile version of 2018" — similar timing of lows and countertrend rallies, but smaller magnitude — and notes 2018's breakdown didn't occur until November, with the eventual capitulation event resetting on-chain indicators in Q4.

## Predictions made
- BTC strength/rally window ends within 2–4 weeks from now, giving way to weakness into August-September.
- BTC likely retests the ~57k February low, and possibly goes a bit lower later in the year, to fully reset on-chain indicators — contingent on happening before year-end.
- S&P 500 expected to top in August or September, followed by a 10-20% drop that would drag Bitcoin into a market cycle bottom.
- If BTC has not broken down by the end of the year, Cowen says he would pivot to viewing the bull market as already resumed (time-based capitulation).
