## TL;DR
He argues traders should always pull their initial investment out once up 2x or more, so they're playing risk-free and less likely to sell emotionally.

## Main points
1. Once up 2x or more, take your initial investment out — this puts you "risk-free" and removes the emotional stress of holding, making you feel "more confident."
2. He argues this behavior is also good for a coin/community collectively: the more holders who have already secured their initial investment and are riding risk-free, the more successful that coin can be, since those holders are less affected by price outcomes and less prone to panic-driven selling. He frames holding a meme coin post-initial-recoup as "owning digital real estate" on the chain.
