# BTC Cycle Theory

Cowen's framework that Bitcoin and broader risk assets follow an approximately four-year cycle of bull-top, bear-bottom, and accumulation phases. He frames the cycle as macro-driven rather than halving-driven, citing parallel equity-market cycle precedents going back to the 1950s.

## Current Thesis

BTC remains stuck between the bear market resistance band above and the 200-week moving average below, with each successive test punching slightly further through the range — a tightening squeeze Cowen reads as the market groping toward its next real move rather than as a sign the bottom is already in. His seasonal scaffolding is unchanged: 2026 continues to pattern-match 2018 (now explicitly "a less volatile version of 2018"), with a brief July rally (already ~9% this month) expected to reverse into weakness within two to four weeks and be given back in August/September, mirroring the 2018/2022 midterm-year script. He still expects the final cycle low to arrive later in the year alongside a 10-20% stock-market correction, with on-chain indicators only partially reset and a retest of the ~57k February low (possibly slightly lower) needed to fully clear them — though he now attaches an explicit fallback: if BTC hasn't broken down by year-end, he'll pivot to declaring the bull market already resumed via time-based capitulation.

## Key Evidence

- Bitcoin is ping-ponging between the bear market resistance band and the 200-week MA, with each fake-out through either band going slightly further, showing the range tightening ahead of an eventual breakout or breakdown. (JY_wY8XXjYU)
- Midterm-year July returns line up across cycles (2026 ~+9% so far, 2022 +20%, 2018 +40%, 2014 slightly negative), supporting Cowen's pattern that July is a relief month typically given back in August/September, as in 2018 and 2022. (JY_wY8XXjYU)
- ROI-from-low day-count: cycle on day 1333 vs. prior bottoms at day 1432/1436 (~100 days out, pointing to October, December if the bear runs as long as 2014's); complemented by the ROI-from-peak-to-low chart (day 283 vs. 364/377/406 for prior post-halving-year bears). (oj2n8nkTTTE, sy6AxBbgico, XQN1M-sIuGs)
- 2026's price action pattern-matches 2018/2022 at scale (February low, higher low in March/April, May lower high at the 200-day MA, June/early-July lower low, July relief rally historically given back in August/September); Cowen extends this same 2018-vs-2022 fork to altcoin behavior. (sy6AxBbgico, 8TmOvGK235I, oj2n8nkTTTE, rxekpAAHwRs, XQN1M-sIuGs)
- Social participation reading of ~0.25 currently matches July 2018 rather than July 2022's ~0.5; a dedicated video expanded this into a full social-collapse case (YouTube views down ~10x since 2021, subscriber growth down from 50-60K/day to ~285/day, X follower growth down to 1/20th of 2021 levels), with social interest expected to keep declining before bottoming in the next 6-12 months. (XQN1M-sIuGs, OZAQDVkVuG8, UIv9IQ4uXEA)
- Every prior bear market bottom (2014, 2018, 2022) featured a volume capitulation spike that reset on-chain metrics; on-chain indicators remain only partially reset now, with a retest of the ~57k February low (or slightly lower) seen as needed to complete the reset. (psz9FwqWGns, g4FiHz14W14, 1LI9wfqxOmc, 8TmOvGK235I, XQN1M-sIuGs, JY_wY8XXjYU)
