## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin trades around 63.5k, sitting almost exactly at the 200-week moving average (~63.7k), just below it after closing last week at 63,500 vs. a 63,551 average.
2. The bear market resistance band (~69k) is trending down while the 200-week moving average (~63.7k) is trending up — Cowen argues these two lines will converge and force a decisive breakout or breakdown within the next couple of months, before year-end.
3. For on-chain indicators like the NVRV Z-score to fully reset (bottom out below zero), Bitcoin likely needs another move lower later in the year; it's close but not there yet.
4. Historical analogues: in 2022, Bitcoin bounced off the 200-week MA, had a green week in early August, then suffered a larger drawdown; in 2018, the drop began right as August started.
5. In 2022, Bitcoin never even reached the bear market resistance band before the August correction hit, and didn't tag that band again until October — by then the band had fallen well below August's price levels, illustrating that "getting back up" isn't guaranteed.
6. Every prior midterm year has been negative in August: -15% (2022), -15% (2018), -18% (2014), -5% (2010); July, by contrast, is typically green (July 2022 +20%, July 2018 +40%, this July +7%), consistent with a "window of strength" in July giving way to a "window of weakness" starting mid-to-late August.
7. Cowen reiterates short-term price action is a random walk (geometric Brownian motion) and he won't promise exact timing, but expects volatility to pick up sharply once the band/MA squeeze resolves, similar to prior cycle patterns (2014, 2018, 2022).
8. He dismisses critics who argue that widely-anticipated setups (Feb lows, Q4 tops, "sell in May") won't happen simply because everyone expects them — noting these patterns ("sell in May," April/May local tops) have kept playing out as expected.
9. The ITC conference (Investing Through the Cycles, Miami, Nov 20–22) is timed for late November because Cowen expects the market cycle bottom to be in, or very close to being in, by then.

## Predictions made
- A decisive break of either the bear market resistance band (~69k) or the 200-week moving average (~63.7k) will be forced within the next couple of months, and by year-end at the latest.
- Bitcoin likely heads back down below 60k at some point, tied to on-chain indicators needing a further reset later this year (no exact week given).
- Window of weakness expected to open by mid-to-late August 2026, following a July "window of strength," consistent with prior midterm-year seasonality (August has been negative in every prior midterm year: 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022).
- Cowen expects the market cycle bottom to be in, or very close to being in, by the end of November 2026.
