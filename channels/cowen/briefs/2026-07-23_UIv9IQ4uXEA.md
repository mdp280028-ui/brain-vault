## TL;DR
Cowen argues Bitcoin's social interest and engagement metrics (YouTube views/subscribers, X followers, Google Trends, Wikipedia views) have collapsed to levels far below 2021 and even below the prior 2018 cycle, and he sees this apathy as confirming the current cycle mirrors 2018's market structure.

## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin is trading around $64,000 with a pervasive sense of market apathy despite any price gains.
2. Social interest is currently about 0.25, roughly half of the last bear-market reading (~0.5), and matches the 2018 level of about 0.25.
3. Market structure is mirroring 2018: support at 60k in 2026 parallels support at 6k in 2018, with a February low swept in late June/early July in both cycles, lower highs in May, and higher lows in late March/early April.
4. Social risk is built from Google Trends, Coinbase app ranking, YouTube subscribers/views, and follower counts of Bitcoin/crypto/exchange/layer-one analysts on X.
5. YouTube views across tracked crypto channels averaged 3-4 million/day at the 2021 peak versus only ~358,000/day now — roughly a 10x drop over five years.
6. YouTube subscriber growth across these channels has fallen from 50,000-60,000/day in 2021 to about 285/day now.
7. X follower growth for crypto/Bitcoin analysts has dropped from over 100,000/day in 2021 to about 4,000/day now (1/20th); layer-one project followers fell from over 100,000/day to about 8,000/day (less than 10%).
8. The advance-decline index of the top 100 cryptocurrencies has been declining since 2021, consistent with the drop in social interest; many altcoins never made new all-time highs this cycle and are now below their 2022 lows.
9. Bitcoin dominance excluding stablecoins continues trending higher because there is little capital/participation left to rotate into altcoins.
10. Crypto asset-weighted sentiment and Wikipedia page views for Bitcoin have also been trending down since 2021 and never picked up meaningfully this cycle, unlike 2017 and 2021.

## Predictions made
- Cowen expects social interest to keep declining before it eventually bottoms, possibly later this year or early next year, and hopes to see a low form sometime in the next 6-12 months.
