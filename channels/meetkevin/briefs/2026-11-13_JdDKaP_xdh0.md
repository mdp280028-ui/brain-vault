## TL;DR
Kevin says hot PPI, hawkish Fed speakers and no Iran deal make a December hike near certain, with about 3.55 hikes now priced through September 2027. He calls this bearish for risk assets in the short term, though it creates buy-the-dip chances.

## Main points
1. PPI came in hot. Core PPI ex food, energy and trade rose 0.5% m/m and 4.9% y/y (versus 4.8% prior). Final demand services drove the hot read, and 80% of that came from truck freight transportation (+3.9%). He says this shows oil flowing into core prices. Headline PPI (6.5% y/y vs 6.6%, 0.3% m/m vs 0.4%) looks better only because it is measured from the late-October oil dip. Prior m/m prints were revised 0.1 hotter.
2. Fed speakers: Goulsby said "we are not on track" to 2% and the Fed must "act aggressively". He blamed AI demand, Iran and tariff inflation that has not come down, and said the labor market is stable or tightening. Bowman said an Iran resolution is needed to avoid hikes, and that oil may stay elevated even after a deal. Kevin disagrees, saying Gulf exports are already above pre-war levels, so oil could plummet on a deal.
3. Iran: Trump is reportedly telling Vance that no deal is needed, since the election is over and the US has Iran's money and the Strait of Hormuz. Iran says it will engage "without limits". The IAEA raised proliferation concern at Isfahan, with no access since June 2025. Kevin says there is no reason for Trump to care about a deal now. Oil is back at $96 from about $89.90.
4. Pricing: 1.1 hikes are priced for December (about an 11% chance of 50bp on Dec 9), and 3.55 hikes through September 2027, up from 1.9 a week and a half ago. Bitcoin is under $70,000 and the 10-year is 5.147% (below the 5.27% lid, versus 4.9% when a deal looked close). The 2-year is flat at about 28 and is not in shock territory.
5. Tariffs: Bessent said he hopes for deals with Mexico and Canada soon. Kevin mocks this, since it was promised within 90 days of April 2025. Bessent also blamed Lutnik's Commerce Department for failing on chip export controls.
