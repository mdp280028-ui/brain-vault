# Evidence log: Global Interest Rate Comparison

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-08-09 (Ju6Q-D9GZZU): Cowen notes the 2-year Treasury yield has moved above the Fed funds rate, a configuration that historically has preceded the Fed cutting to catch up to the yield rather than the reverse, citing the 1990s as precedent. He separately tracks the US-minus-Japan interest rate differential as a recession leading indicator, since sharp drops in that differential have preceded past downturns — though his overall macro recession risk dashboard currently reads just 0.16, a low level.
