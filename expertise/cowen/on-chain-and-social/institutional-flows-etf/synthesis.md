# Institutional Flows & ETF

ETF inflows and outflows, institutional allocation data, and corporate treasury Bitcoin purchases. Cowen tracks institutional flow as a slow-but-durable demand component distinct from retail-driven price action.

## Current Thesis
Cowen now addresses ETF flows directly, though only as a secondary point. He reads Bitcoin spot ETF outflows as a sign that liquidity leaves crypto around massive IPOs: about $306M went out on Nov 18, into the Anthropic IPO. He ties this to his SpaceX analog, where outflows stalled on IPO day, then resumed and coincided with a lower BTC low weeks later. He expects a BTC local low near the December 9 FOMC meeting and a bounce into 2027. Corporate treasury buying and institutional allocation data are still unaddressed, so the seed framing of slow, durable institutional demand is not yet tested against his own commentary.

- Bitcoin ETF flows showed large outflows into the Anthropic IPO, about $306M out on Nov 18, and Cowen says liquidity leaves crypto around massive IPOs (ixMSi_KX89s).
