## TL;DR
Kevin says X's creator monetization program is being killed and replaced with a stricter "original content" rewards program, and he thinks this is a smart move by Elon Musk/X that will improve content quality and help X sell more advertising.

## Main points
1. X's existing revenue-sharing program retires effective September 7th (final payout September 11th), replaced September 8th by an "Original Content Creators Rewards" program requiring ID verification and other eligibility checks.
2. Backstory: on June 30th, former X exec Nikita (who stepped down from X shortly before the SpaceX lockup) ran a 3% experiment removing the top 30 highest-paid revenue-share accounts from the For You timeline; both time spent and daily active usage went up, suggesting high-earning repost/quote-tweet accounts were degrading the platform rather than helping it.
3. New rules only pay out on "qualified impressions" — unique impressions from premium users, on the home timeline where at least 50% of the post is visible; paid/promoted/artificially generated/fraudulent impressions don't count, and payouts are capped at $30. Copied content, minimally-modified reposts, and news-aggregation-with-no-new-perspective are explicitly excluded from payment.
4. New eligibility bar: must be Premium/Premium+/Business subscriber, have 500,000+ home timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days (rolling), have 500+ verified followers, and actively post original content — status is continuously re-tested, not a one-time qualification.
5. Kevin cites SpaceX's (X's parent, per earnings filing page 44) Grok/X subscription revenue rising to $258 million, but ad revenue falling from $426 million to $367 million (a $59 million decline) — he argues subscriber growth is being offset by weaker advertising, which he ties back to the platform being flooded with low-value repost/quote-tweet content.
