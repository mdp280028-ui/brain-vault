# Social Engagement Risk

Social-media engagement metrics (mentions, Google Trends, sentiment indices) used as a contrarian signal. High retail engagement coincides with cyclical tops; apathy coincides with cyclical bottoms in Cowen's framework.

## Current Thesis
Cowen's social interest/risk metric remains stuck at ~0.25, matching July 2018 rather than the hotter 2022 (~0.5) or 2021-peak readings, and he backs this with a wider battery of collapsing engagement proxies (YouTube views/subscribers, X follower growth, Wikipedia views, crypto sentiment) that have fallen roughly an order of magnitude since 2021 — a point he's now reiterated in a short standalone clip isolating the YouTube-views data alone. For Bitcoin, he reads this apathy as confirming a 2018-style market-structure analog (60k support paralleling 2018's 6k) and has attached his first explicit directional call from this thesis — a bearish 2-year BTC outlook — rather than treating the apathy as merely "not yet topped." Applied to Ethereum, he still treats the same low reading as a warning rather than reassurance, since thin social participation means less buying support to cushion ETH into a possible deeper-than-2022 drawdown, with a Fed rate-hike scare in September–October flagged as a catalyst for a fresh test of the ETH/BTC support band. He expects social interest to keep declining before eventually bottoming sometime in the next 6-12 months, and separately notes that dwindling participation is also starving altcoins of rotation, keeping BTC dominance (ex-stables) trending higher.

## Key Evidence
- Social interest/risk metric currently ~0.25, matching July 2018 levels rather than 2022's ~0.5 or the 2021 peak (prior; reaffirmed OZAQDVkVuG8, UIv9IQ4uXEA)
- Crypto YouTube channel view counts have collapsed from 3-4 million/day at the 2021 peak to ~358K/day now (~10x drop over five years), with subscriber growth falling from 50-60K/day to ~285/day (UIv9IQ4uXEA; restated as a standalone clip in Z4MEkBsa6Vk; prior cited ~350K/day vs. ~1M/day in 2022)
- X follower growth for crypto/Bitcoin analysts dropped from 100K+/day in 2021 to ~4,000/day (1/20th), and layer-one project followers fell from 100K+/day to ~8,000/day (UIv9IQ4uXEA)
- Social risk index composite (Google Trends, Coinbase app ranking, YouTube views/subscribers, X follower growth) plus advance-decline index, BTC dominance ex-stables, crypto sentiment, and Wikipedia views all corroborate a multi-year decline in engagement since 2021 (UIv9IQ4uXEA)
- Low social participation cited as a specific reason ETH's drawdown could exceed 2022's pullback, since there's less retail buying support to hold levels (OZAQDVkVuG8)
- Drying social interest, combined with a possible Fed rate hike/scare in September–October, flagged as a catalyst for one more test of the ETH/BTC bull market support band (OZAQDVkVuG8)
