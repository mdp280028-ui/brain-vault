# Macro Headwinds Crypto

## Current Thesis

Cowen argues Bitcoin's weakness is macro- and cycle-driven rather than political or idiosyncratic. Rising inflation, a deteriorating unemployment trend, and a dollar grinding back toward 105–106 have suppressed risk assets through the Biden and Trump-second-term eras alike, and he now overlays this with an intact four-year cycle that places the current bear market in its final ~30%. He draws a direct analogy to 2019 — apathy-driven top, no altcoin rotation, top preceding the end of QT by about two months — and expects both the macro drag and the cycle-driven downtrend to persist for a few more months before a bottom lands between late September and mid-December 2026, clearing the way for the next bull leg into 2027.

## Key Evidence

- Biden's and Trump's second-term BTC returns are nearly identical at the ~524-day mark (-43.8% vs -41.1%), both dramatically weaker than Obama's second term and Trump's first term — Cowen attributes the gap to shared macro conditions, not politics. (prior)
- The DXY topped in January 2017 and again in January 2025, then bottomed in early 2018 and early 2026 respectively, and is now grinding higher in both cases; Cowen forecasts it returning to the 105–106 area as a continued headwind for crypto later in 2026. (prior)
- Bitcoin topped roughly two months before QT ended in both 2019 and 2025, both years saw three Fed rate cuts, no rotation into altcoins, and a top driven by apathy (falling social interest, rising BTC dominance) rather than euphoria, reinforcing the macro-parallel case. (CmGkfyot8qY)
- Bitcoin is on day 1,354 of a historical 1,430–1,440 day cycle-low pattern and is currently tracking the 200-week moving average, the same breakdown sequence (50-week → 100-week → 200-week) seen in the last three bear markets. (CmGkfyot8qY)
- Cowen's base case bottom window is late September through mid-December 2026: "that would probably be like late September through mid December is normally when you would expect it to bottom." [12:44] (CmGkfyot8qY)
