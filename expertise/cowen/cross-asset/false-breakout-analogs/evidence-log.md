# Evidence log: False Breakout Analogs

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-11-12 (QcJlPjcOZjw): Cowen rejects the 2019 and 2023 analogies that bulls used after the break above the previous high. He argues a midterm year makes it look more like a false breakout, as in 2019, 2015, 2020 and March 2022, which turned difficult afterward. He also compares the post-first-rate-hike pattern in March 2022 to now: a rally, fading, then a fall. He says of the higher-low alternative: "This is not my main scenario right now." [08:45]

- 2026-11-16 (HcruM-mYz5E): After the false breakout above the ~$83K highs, Cowen says price tends to return to the lows quickly, and BTC is now ~65K after a 12%+ weekly drop. He compares the setup to false-breakout capitulations that fell 30–50% in one to two weeks (2015, 2019–2020, March) and to 2018, when the final bottom came in mid-December around the FOMC meeting. He dismisses inverted head-and-shoulders and higher-low talk as patterns that fail more often than they work. "if Bitcoin doesn't bounce here, then to me that means we're very close to the final candle of surrender, right?" [10:35]
