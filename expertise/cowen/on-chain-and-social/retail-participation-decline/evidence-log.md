# Evidence log: Retail Participation Decline

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-07-27 (lfYYC7qxDQ8): Cowen notes that retail participation is conspicuously missing this cycle compared to the 2017-style euphoric blow-off that drove alt season, pointing to declining YouTube viewership across crypto channels as evidence, which he attributes to retail fatigue with alt-market grift and rug pulls. He ties this to his view that the cycle topped on apathy rather than euphoria: "The main reason it feels different again is because we topped on apathy rather than euphoria." [04:11]
