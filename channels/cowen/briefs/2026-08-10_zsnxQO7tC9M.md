## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin is trading around $64,000–65,000; Cowen says historically midterm years always play out the same way — Bitcoin sells off at the very end of the post-halving year.
2. Bitcoin typically forms "a low" (not necessarily "the low") in the summer — cites summer 2022, summer 2026, and summer 2018 as examples.
3. After the summer low, volatility dries up and interest in crypto fades until some event near year-end causes a final drop that "wakes everyone back up" and kicks off the next bull market.
4. Social interest in Bitcoin is currently at 0.2, less than half of where it was 4 years ago at the same point in the cycle (pre-FTX, 0.4–0.5), but matches August 2018 levels (also 0.2).
5. On-chain indicators like the NVRVZ (NVT/RVT-style Z-score) haven't given an "all-clear" — that score normally goes below zero at Bitcoin bottoms and hasn't done so yet, suggesting price could still go lower even though Cowen has personally bought some.
6. Bitcoin has been oscillating between the 200-week moving average and the bear market resistance band for months; as the year progresses this range will force a directional decision — Cowen leans toward a breakdown since that's typical near the end of midterm years.
7. Cowen expects the low to be in, or very close to being in, by late November; October is his most likely month, though it could come as early as late September or as late as December.
8. Historical analogs for the bounce off the summer low: in 2018 the bounce lasted only into late July; in 2022 it lasted into mid-August — Cowen believes Bitcoin hasn't yet overstayed its welcome at current levels.
9. "Crunch time" historically shows up in late August through October, sometimes stretching into November/December (2015 was an outlier, bottoming in January).
10. The ITC conference is scheduled for November 20th–22nd (main day November 21st) specifically timed to follow this expected event; ticket prices rise September 1st.

## Predictions made
- BTC: low likely in by late November, with October the single most likely month (could be as early as late September or as late as December).
- BTC: leans toward a breakdown out of the 200-week MA / bear market resistance band range "near the end of midterm years," though he says this isn't certain.
