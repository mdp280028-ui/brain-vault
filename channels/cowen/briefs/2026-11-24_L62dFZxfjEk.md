## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin set a lower low in Q4 of the midterm year. He says this is normal: capitulations historically occur at the end of midterm years (sometimes early the following year, as in 2015). It only breaks models that were too optimistic.
2. He says all models are wrong, including his own. People who said the four-year cycle was wrong or that Bitcoin had "failed" are reinterpreting price action to fit their own models. The chart behavior is what Bitcoin always does.
3. Metals analogy: gold and silver each had a low in the first quarter and in the summer. Then they rebounded, and everyone called the bottom, but they made a lower low and capitulated. Silver rebounded in early November, then capitulated. Metals are still falling, and he says they should bottom first.
4. Realized price is about $53K. Bitcoin historically falls below it in bear markets (2014–15, 2018 around Nov 20, 2022). Critics who say it no longer matters could have said the same in 2022, 2018 and 2014, and it fell below each time. So a further drop below is still possible.
5. Historical gravity on Bitcoin: it usually falls to the realized price, usually keeps falling after the first rate hike in a hiking cycle and after a September midterm-year rate hike, and usually bottoms after metals (gold bottomed in Sept 2022, then BTC in Nov; gold in Aug 2018, then BTC in Dec; gold Oct 2008, then S&P Mar 2009).
6. He is not saying a bottom is impossible, and odds have risen given the lower low in Q4 of a midterm year. But he warns against people who said the low was in all year. His strategy is DCA in the second half of the midterm cycle and buying at risk levels. No one will time the exact bottom.
7. Bear markets fool both bulls and bears. He was wrong about the size of the rally above the May high. Bear markets rise for a long time and then fall quickly to new lows. He says the market structure changed on Oct 10, 2025.
8. If BTC has not made a new low by 2027, some bears will turn bulls and a bottom becomes more credible. Until then, he sees the window of weakness as open. Past analogs are March 2022 (break the low, then continue to capitulate), 2015 and 2022.
9. Risk in the network is 0.165, and he says it usually bottoms lower. The whale activity index shows no accumulation like at past lows: it spiked to 0.7–0.9 at the 2014, 2018 and 2022 lows, but only 0.4–0.5 at the February low, about 0.22 in summer, and about 0.1 now.
10. Social interest is very low, resembling the 2018 and 2020 sell-offs more than 2022. Apathetic tops may lead to more violent bear markets with false breakouts in both directions.

## Predictions made
- The window of weakness for BTC may stay open for a while longer. A confirmed low is more plausible only if no new low is made by 2027.
- Bitcoin's bottom likely comes after metals bottom, per historical precedent.
