## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Cowen's base case: the Fed holds rates constant at tomorrow's (July 29th) FOMC meeting but raises rates in September — it would be unprecedented in recent history for the Fed to start hiking in summer, since the last two cutting cycles (2024, 2025) both began cuts in September.
2. He expects the 10-year yield to head back toward its October 2023 high and the 30-year yield to break through the 5.1-5.2% level it's been rejected at since 2023, since the 30-year has already been making higher lows against that ceiling.
3. Initial jobless claims just printed 187,000, the lowest level he says he's seen in decades, and the unemployment rate (4.2%) has been trending down since November 2025 — signaling the labor market is fine, so the bond market's real concern is inflation, not employment.
4. Inflation rose from ~2.4% to ~4.1% before dropping back to 3.4-3.5%; he flags energy (XLE) as a risk that could reaccelerate inflation if it continues to firm from its ~52-53 print.
5. FedWatch tool currently prices only a ~29.4% chance of a rate hike tomorrow; he notes the Fed tends to follow market-implied odds when they're this lopsided.
6. The two-year yield (which the Fed tracks/follows) is now above the Fed funds rate, meaning policy is arguably no longer sufficiently restrictive — the market expects the Fed to raise rates to 4.25% by December, which lines up with where the two-year yield already sits (~4.1-4.2%).
7. His scenario chain: Fed holds in July → bond vigilantes push the long end higher → stock market correction starts in the August-September window → Fed delivers a couple of rate hikes → Bitcoin drops further into its market-cycle bottom, which normally falls in Q4 of midterm years.
8. He cites 2022 (correction starting August), 2018 (correction starting September, also a midterm year with Trump as president), 2014 (correction starting ~September), and 2023 (correction starting late July amid rising long-end yields) as precedent for 10-20% stock corrections tied to this setup.
9. Draws a parallel to the mid-1990s dot-com era, when the Fed resumed hiking after a cutting cycle and hiked seven or eight times before "breaking something" — implying one hike alone wouldn't be a major shock to the economy, though it would hurt speculative assets like altcoins.
10. On Bitcoin dominance: when stablecoins are excluded, BTC dominance is still rising; the appearance of stalled dominance is because stablecoin dominance roughly doubled (from ~6% to ~12-13%) since October 2025, reflecting a "flight to safety" driven by continued restrictive monetary policy.

## Predictions made
- Fed holds rates constant at the July 29th FOMC meeting, then raises rates in September.
- 10-year yield heads back up toward its October 2023 high; 30-year yield breaks through the 5.1-5.2% resistance level.
- Market-implied Fed funds rate target of 4.25% by December.
- Stock market (S&P) correction of 10-20% starting in the August-September timeframe (back half of the midterm year).
- Bitcoin drops further to a market-cycle bottom in Q4, the back half of the back half of the midterm year.
