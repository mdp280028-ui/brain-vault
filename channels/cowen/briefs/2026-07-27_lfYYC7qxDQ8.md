## TL;DR
Cowen says Bitcoin's July rally is following the same seasonal script as 2018 (low in Feb, higher low in spring, sweep in late June/early July, rally into the bear market resistance band), and expects rejection there followed by a pullback into August-September before a possible final drop and market cycle bottom later in the post-halving year.

## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin has traded rangebound between 63.7K and 67K over the past week, with not much net movement (opened week at 64.7K, currently ~64.7K).
2. The July rally seen in midterm years continues: 2022 was up 20% in July, 2018 up almost 40%, and 2026 is up about 10% so far this July.
3. In 2022, 2018, and 2014, the July strength was followed by red August and September months — Cowen expects the same pattern to repeat.
4. 2018 remains his preferred structural analog: February low, higher low in late March/early April, a swept low in late June/early July, then a rally back up to the bear market resistance band by late July/early August — and he says 2026 is following this exact playbook every month this year.
5. He expects Bitcoin to get rejected near the resistance band (as in 2018) and pull back in the August-September window, with Q4 being the historical period when on-chain indicators fully reset.
6. He argues 2026 only "feels" different from prior cycles because this cycle topped on apathy rather than euphoria (topped at ~126K vs. an implied ~200K if it matched 2018's magnitude), not because the structure is actually different.
7. Retail participation is notably absent this cycle (unlike the 2017-style euphoric blow-off that fueled alt season) — evidenced by declining YouTube viewership across crypto channels, which he attributes to retail fatigue with alt-market grift and rug pulls.
8. Historically, Bitcoin hasn't durably broken above the bear market resistance band until well into the post-halving year: January (prior cycle), March (cycle before that), June/October 2015 (cycle before that) — implying a long wait before a durable breakout is typical.
9. He concedes the cycle bottom could already be in, but thinks if so, price would likely stay capped rather than rally much, due to lingering fear of a final Q4 drop keeping selling pressure alive.
10. Overall guess: Bitcoin gets rejected near resistance in late July/early August, comes back down, maybe bounces again, and later in the year the market determines whether a final drop/cycle bottom occurs.

## Predictions made
- Bitcoin likely gets rejected near the bear market resistance band in late July/early August, similar to 2018.
- Bitcoin likely pulls back into the August-September timeframe following the July rally.
- Q4 is the historically likely window for a final drop and full on-chain indicator reset / market cycle bottom, though not certain.
- Durable break above the bear market resistance band historically hasn't occurred until well into the post-halving year, suggesting any strong sustained upside is still far off.
