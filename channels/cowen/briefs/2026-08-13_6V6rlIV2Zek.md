## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin trading around $63,000; Cowen frames the next 60 days (mid-August to mid-October) as historically one of the weakest periods for Bitcoin in midterm years.
2. Seasonality data: in 2022, 2018, and 2026 so far, Bitcoin was red in May/June, green in July — the pattern suggests August/September could be red again, though he notes seasonality only "works maybe about 70% of the time."
3. Going back to 2010, midterm years average a ~10-11% drop in August and ~8% in September — from current prices that would put BTC around 56K, and further down into the low 50s if both hit.
4. ROI-from-low cycle analysis: prior cycle lows occurred on day 1432 and day 1436 from cycle start; we're currently on day 1360, meaning 60 days from now (day 1420) lines up within a week or two of where prior cycle lows occurred.
5. Structural comparison to 2018: same low-in-February, higher-low-in-spring, lower-low-in-summer pattern, just "10x higher" (60K now vs 6K then); this cycle topped on apathy rather than euphoria (no altcoin rotation), and social interest metrics (~0.2) resemble 2018 far more than 2022 (~0.5).
6. Historical four-year-cycle lows have crept earlier each cycle (January → December → November), making October a "candidate month" — Cowen estimates roughly 50% odds the low lands in October versus 50% spread across all other months combined.
7. Bear market duration comparison: last two bear markets ran 52-54 weeks, pointing to a low around Oct 5-19; the 2019 bear market (pandemic-shortened) ran 37 weeks; the bear market before that ran 59 weeks, which would push a low to the week of November 23rd — notably the same week as his ITC conference (Nov 20-22).
8. Alternative scenario: Bitcoin could go sideways for months like in 2018 (rallied to resistance in July, chopped through Aug-Oct, then broke down later) — volatility could simply dry up rather than drop immediately, delaying the low toward November.
9. Notes 2026's rally to resistance was much weaker than 2018's (16% vs ~50% from the June/July low), which he reads as a sign this cycle could play out faster with fewer participants.
10. Overall odds framing: low most likely between late September and mid-December, with October his lead candidate ("October to October makes the most amount of sense to me"); watching whether on-chain indicators (MVRVZ score, on-chain risk composite including PEL multiple, transaction fees, market-cap-to-thermocap ratio) reset below their typical thresholds as confirmation.

## Predictions made
- BTC: seasonal drop of ~10-11% in August and ~8% in September (midterm-year historical average) would put price around 56K, then low 50s if both occur — no fixed timeframe commitment, framed as historical base rate.
- BTC: cycle low most likely to occur between late September and mid-December 2026, with October as the leading candidate month (~50% odds vs. all other months combined); if bear market extends to 59 weeks, low pushes to week of November 23rd.
