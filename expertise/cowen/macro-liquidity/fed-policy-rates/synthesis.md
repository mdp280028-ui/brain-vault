# Fed Policy & Rates

The Federal Reserve's policy-rate decisions, FOMC meeting cycles, rate-cut and rate-hike paths, and the politics around Fed leadership transitions (Powell, Warsh, successors). Cowen treats Fed policy as the primary macro driver for crypto and equities.

## Current Thesis

Heading into the September 16th FOMC, Cowen frames the decision as roughly a coin flip (57% hike / 43% hold), and argues either outcome still points toward a correction: a hike could reignite fear that the hiking cycle is restarting, while a hold could still see the 10-year (4.6%) and 30-year (back to October 2023 levels) grind higher and pressure risk assets regardless. He now leans on midterm-year seasonality as the timing anchor, sharpened by a specific historical callout: the 2014 and 2018 corrections both started right around September 19th-21st — within days of a Fed meeting — which he reads as precedent for the September 16th date this cycle. The core causal chain is unchanged: rate-hike fear and rising long-end yields pressure stocks into a second-half correction, which in turn ushers in Bitcoin's market cycle bottom, tracking the market's second, later-year correction (as in 2014/2018/2022) rather than the one already seen earlier in 2026. His conviction on the equity-timing call has softened slightly in this brief (SPX months estimate ↓2, down from ↓4 previously) even as the coin-flip framing and the seasonality logic itself remain unchanged; he continues to hedge on depth, from a shallow 2018-style hold to a low-confidence S&P-retests-6,000/BTC-below-60K scenario.

## Key Evidence

- Fed held rates at the July 29th FOMC (3.50-3.75% target); the September 16th meeting is now priced by Cowen at roughly 57% hike / 43% hold, a genuine coin flip rather than a settled base case (3yiYSG8sOFE, cmgvWLCes0s)
- The 2014 and 2018 midterm-year corrections both began within days of a Fed meeting — September 19th and September 21st respectively — which Cowen flags as direct precedent for the September 16th FOMC this cycle (cmgvWLCes0s)
- Cowen notes 2024 and 2025 rate cuts both first occurred in September, and argues the Fed may prefer to act in September rather than right before midterms or waiting until December (3yiYSG8sOFE)
- 10-year yield at 4.6% and rising, 30-year already back to October 2023 levels — cited as pressure on risk assets even in a "hold" scenario (3yiYSG8sOFE, prior)
- Three of twelve FOMC members dissented in favor of an immediate hike at the July meeting, and Warsh has abandoned forward guidance ("play the ball and not the referee"), with the two-year yield trading above the Fed funds rate signaling policy is no longer restrictive (q2QLLihl6cc, prior)
- Historical midterm-year corrections (Sept 19th 2014 ~10%, Sept 21st 2018 ~20%, Aug 16th 2022 ~20%) all started mid-to-late in the year and took months to bottom, underpinning the Q4 2026 Bitcoin cycle-bottom framework (3yiYSG8sOFE, cmgvWLCes0s, prior)
