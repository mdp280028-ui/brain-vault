## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Gold has rebounded slightly off its lows; in gold bull markets, price often comes back to test the 20-month SMA / 21-month EMA "bull market support band," and has even dropped below it before during the 2000s and 1970s bull runs.
2. Cowen acknowledges he could be wrong and that a macro top could be in, but doesn't think it is.
3. Gold's 2026 year-to-date ROI is tracking almost identically to 2022 (slightly underperforming) and similarly to 2018, both of which saw gold find relief around this point in the year before one more period of later weakness.
4. Averaging all prior midterm years, gold tends to bottom in early July; Cowen frames the broader bottoming window as June through October.
5. From its high, gold has corrected about 30%, comparable to 20-30% drawdowns seen within prior gold bull markets that did not mark the end of the bull run.
6. Cowen expects at least a bounce in the "not-so-distant future," and if gold drops once more, he expects it to front-run a stock market correction (which he doesn't see starting until "probably sometime in late September") and bottom sooner than stocks.
7. Any further gold weakness would likely coincide with the US dollar (DXY) moving higher; Cowen compares 2026's dollar action to 2018's dollar path under Trump's first term — falling, then sideways, then "begrudgingly" grinding higher.
8. Comparing gold's performance across presidential terms (Biden and Trump's first term), gold was weak in the second year of the term but picked back up in years three and four.
9. Gold is described as being in a "massive consolidation period" in 2026, similar to a consolidation seen in 2022 (move up, consolidation, move up, consolidation pattern); Cowen expects one final move up that could mark a more significant global top later, potentially near the end of the decade.
10. On average, gold rises about 13% through pre-election years (which 2027 would be), reinforcing Cowen's expectation that gold will carve out a bottom this year and use it as support to move higher into late 2026 and 2027.

## Predictions made
- Gold's main bottoming window for this midterm-year cycle is June through October 2026.
- If stocks correct, Cowen doesn't expect that correction to start until "probably sometime in late September."
- Gold could see one more period of weakness (potentially into mid-August, per the 2022 year-to-date overlay) before resuming its uptrend.
- Gold expected to rise roughly 13% on average through the 2027 pre-election year.
