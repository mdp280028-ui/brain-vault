## Current Thesis

SpaceX has become Cowen's template for how mega-IPOs behave: a ~50% surge on launch ($149 to $225), then a ~53% drop that offers a better entry than chasing the debut. He is now applying it to the Anthropic IPO (priced $83, ~$1.5T fully diluted), expecting a surge on launch and a pullback within a couple of weeks. He also reads SpaceX as a market and Bitcoin analog: markets rallied and then made a slightly lower low about 32 trading days later, and BTC made a slightly lower low about 18–20 days after the IPO, which maps to a possible BTC local low near the December 9 FOMC. Post-drop outcomes vary (Tesla 2010 made a higher low and a macro low; Coinbase, Rivian and Apple made lower lows), so he treats the drop as the buying window rather than the launch.
