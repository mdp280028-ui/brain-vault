## TL;DR
Cowen went live as Bitcoin hit the 21-week EMA (~76.6K) amid an exchange hack and a tech-stock selloff. He says this is the typical midterm-year Q4 dump. It ends either in a fast FTX-style capitulation before the midterms, or after a bounce at the bull market support band, with a final drop right after the midterms. He calls the two equally likely.

## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin is at about 77K, sitting on the 21-week EMA (76,612). The low printed was about 76,642. The bull market support band runs from about 73K to 77K, and the 200-day is about 73K.
2. Cowen sees two roughly equally likely endings. Option one is no support at the band and a fast, FTX-style capitulation that could bottom before the midterms. Option two is a bounce at the band for about a week (like the pre-SpaceX-IPO price action, now compared with the upcoming Anthropic IPO), then a final drop right after the midterms.
3. In the prior midterm years (2014, 2018, 2022), the final lows came right after the midterms. This time is different because Bitcoin has fallen for four weekly candles going into the midterms. In past cycles it held or rose into them.
4. The structure is a fake-out above the prior (May) high. Two weekly closes below that level would convince people it was a fake-out, and then the Q4 low follows. Cowen admits he was wrong that Bitcoin would take out the May high.
5. Cycle-day counts support a bottom soon. Prior cycles bottomed on day 1432 and 1436, and the current day is 1439. He calls this the window for the next few days.
6. Equities are confirming. Google fell about 12% on an earnings miss, Amazon is down 8% after hours, Intel is down 23% after hours, and the Nasdaq is down about 9% with three weeks of red candles. Nasdaq futures are down over 2%. Brazil's EWZ fell 20% in a day. Q1 low, summer low and Q4 low structures appear in many markets.
7. Historical bear-market lows came with a massive volume spike, and this bear market has none. A price-based capitulation below the realized price has happened in every Q4 of a midterm year. Cowen guesses Bitcoin drops toward the realized price or the rainbow chart eventually. The worst case would be a 2011-silver-style path with the low in 2027 or 2028.
8. Crypto participation is down by about an order of magnitude from 2021, whale activity is muted, and the top-100 advance/decline index is still falling. Bitcoin dominance is holding at about 60%, and stablecoin dominance is trending up.
9. Macro: he expects the dollar to rally into December, with a rate hike likely then. He sees oil going higher (Brent about 118 to 120, with a possible new all-time high) because of the Iran war and a stated attack after the midterms. He also expects metals to bottom in the coming weeks, with Bitcoin sweeping its lows against gold and then rallying.
10. Advice: he doesn't keep crypto on exchanges ("like public restrooms") and prefers US dollars to stablecoins. He expects a smaller drawdown than the last cycle's 77% (diminishing losses). He hopes for a rally in 2027.
