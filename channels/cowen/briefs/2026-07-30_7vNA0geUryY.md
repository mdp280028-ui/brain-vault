## Key points (the distilled video)
1. July 2026 is up about 10.5%, tracking the pattern of past midterm-year Julys (2022 up ~20%, 2018 up ~38%), which Cowen says typically act as relief rallies after a June low.
2. In 2022 and 2018, Bitcoin rallied off a June low but the rally faded before or around the bear market resistance band; in 2018 it got close to the 200-day moving average without touching it, while in 2022 it didn't reach either level until much later (November for the resistance band, January for the 200-day MA).
3. 2014 was a partial exception since the cycle low didn't occur until October, though June still marked a higher/stronger local low.
4. Cowen reiterates that 2026's year-to-date Bitcoin ROI closely overlays 2018's, and that overlay suggests the "window of weakness" is starting to reappear right around now.
5. He explicitly says no one can know for certain whether Bitcoin will reach the bear market resistance band or 200-day MA this time.
6. He flags the 10-year Treasury yield turning back up as a bearish analog to 2023, when a rising long end of the curve coincided with Bitcoin turning down from July; he notes the bond market is "starting to revolt" while the Fed isn't cutting rates.
7. Historical monthly returns pattern: in 2022 and 2018 July was green but August/September were red; in 2014 July, August, and September were all red — reinforcing his call for renewed weakness ahead.
8. He expects the next leg down to start within 2-3 weeks, arriving sometime in August or September, potentially extending briefly into August before turning down.
9. If this bear market plays out like prior cycles (using the "ROI from the low" chart), he expects the cycle low to arrive within a few more months, with the market either at or close to a bottom by around November.
10. He recommends a DCA strategy over trying to time the exact bottom, and plugs the ITC conference in Miami, November 20-22.

## Predictions made
- Bitcoin's next "window of weakness" (renewed downside) begins within the next 2-3 weeks, landing in August or September.
- Bear market low expected within a few more months, with market at or near a cycle bottom by around November 2026.
