# Evidence log: Non Farm Payroll Weakness

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-08-09 (Ju6Q-D9GZZU): Non-farm payroll came in at 23,000 versus an expected ~80,000, with year-over-year growth having slowed from ~5% in 2022 to roughly 2% now and margin for error thin after a near-miss into negative territory in February. Despite this, the unemployment rate has actually fallen from 4.5% to 4.1% since November — driven by a shrinking labor force participation rate (62.5% to 61.4%), not stronger hiring. Layoffs remain at pre-pandemic levels and initial claims recently printed ~189,000, the lowest in about 50 years, leading Cowen to note: "as long as initial claims are below 300K, we're just not in a recession" [23:48].
