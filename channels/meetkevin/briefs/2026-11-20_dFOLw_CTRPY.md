## TL;DR
The Nasdaq 100 is red for the year and Anthropic is down 10% after its IPO. Kevin says the best hedges are cash, no debt and real estate, plus a diversified basket of beaten-down stocks. He calls gold and Bitcoin non-hedges under a hawkish Warsh Fed. He is buying the dip in small size.

## Main points
1. Gold is a bad hedge. He cites his Jan 30 call that Warsh as Fed chair is bad for gold. The Fed is shrinking its balance sheet, which brings disinflation. Gold usually soars 3-5 years after money printing. He is bearish from $5,000 and it is now $3,500. He allows a bounce to $4K, but thinks $2,xxx is likelier than $5,xxx. A Democratic money printer after Warsh's term ends (May 2030, to be checked) would be bullish.
2. Put options are dangerous. The VIX is at its highest since Mar 27, so premiums are inflated. You can be right on direction and still lose when volatility compresses. He says to sell options when volatility is high. He suggests selling calls instead of dumping holdings.
3. Cash and no debt are the best hedges. Kevin's company holds about 15% cash, about 3% stocks and 82-83% real estate. He says to pay off the mortgage and car loan even at 2%, because it gives you options. Margin is to be avoided, since margin calls create capitulation bottoms. He calls the margin-driven fear "what debt says."
4. Bonds: he predicted a 10-year peak at 5.27%, and yields are falling as people buy. The 6-month yields 4.4%. He favors a ladder of 6-month, 1-year and 2-year Treasuries over 10-year ones, because 10-year ones carry principal risk if sold early. He expects yields to fall sharply when the AI bubble bursts and the labor market weakens.
5. Real estate is his main hedge. Central Florida (Orlando, Tampa) is still falling on oversupply, so avoid falling knives. He likes supply-constrained, Democratic-led areas like California, where limited building supports prices. It also benefits when rates fall or the AI bubble bursts. He sees no recession and no sharp rate drop soon, and thinks the AI bubble is still ahead of us.
6. Consumer stocks and healthcare were the hedges and are getting stretched. Disney is at $119. Apple is 3-4% below its highs. Cheesecake Factory is a bubble and too risky, since low-income consumers could stop buying. Eli Lilly is getting expensive (he also holds Moderna, bought near $145). Rocket Mortgage would only hedge a rate collapse.
7. Bitcoin is not a hedge. Its beta to the market is about 1, so it falls with stocks. He sees $102K+ in the next AI hardware bubble cycle. He is skeptical of MicroStrategy.
8. Diversify and buy the dip small. He bought this morning from a watchlist of 30-35 names (Nvidia, Marvell, Robinhood, Microsoft, Amazon) but doesn't claim a bottom. Fear of China is overblown. Tax-loss harvesting works best by swapping (for example Nvidia to AMD, or into Cerebras, which he thinks has a great 2027). Goldman says hedge funds are reducing gross positions.
