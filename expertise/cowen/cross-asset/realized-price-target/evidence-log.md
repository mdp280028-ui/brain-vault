# Evidence log: Realized Price Target

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-11-16 (HcruM-mYz5E): With BTC near $65K after a 12%+ weekly drop and a false breakout, Cowen noted a further 17% fall would reach the realized price near $54K. He stressed he is not claiming the price floor is near, only that the bear market is close to ending in time, with a final low likely around the mid-December FOMC meeting. He also warned that people who denied the realized-price risk all year are unlikely to admit a mistake if it plays out. "So what if Bitcoin fell another 17%, right? That would bring it down to the realized price, somewhere around $54,000." [02:50]

- 2026-11-24 (L62dFZxfjEk): Cowen puts the realized price at about $53K and notes Bitcoin has historically fallen below it in bear markets (2014–15, 2018, 2022), so critics saying it no longer matters could have said the same each time. He pairs it with other historical gravity (post-rate-hike weakness and Bitcoin bottoming after metals) and low whale accumulation (~0.1 now vs 0.7–0.9 at past lows) to argue against calling a bottom. As he puts it: "but the realized price is what Bitcoin has historically fallen below." [09:14]
