# Evidence log: Saylor Mstr Bitcoin Selling

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-07-09 (OgvqZ2BiHZw): Extensive panel discussion on Michael Saylor/Strategy: MSTR's mNAV fell below 1, creating a "negative flywheel" for stock issuance, prompting Strategy to sell Bitcoin (~$200M+ in one day, ~3,000 BTC over two weeks) as part of a monetization plan of up to $1.25B to buy back Stretch (STRC) preferred stock and MSTR shares, while still holding 830,000+ BTC. Nick argues ripping off the band-aid is preferable to a prolonged overhang, while Rob notes the bad optics given Saylor's "never sell" reputation: "I think it's still looking dicey. Um, we have to see what happens once he's sold it." [22:43]

- 2026-07-30 (q2QLLihl6cc): Guy explains Strategy's failure to buy Bitcoin dips as driven by cash-flow strain from dividend obligations on its STRC preferred stock, leaving the company to choose between diluting MSTR holders or selling BTC rather than accumulating; he frames this as part of a broader, long-term bullish dynamic where weaker digital-asset-treasury companies keep failing and release Bitcoin back to the market ("treasury companies will bleed to Bitcoin"). Cowen also corrects an earlier claim in the discussion, noting Strategy actually bought at $117K, not "the high."
