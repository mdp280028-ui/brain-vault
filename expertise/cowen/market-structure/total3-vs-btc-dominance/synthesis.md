# TOTAL3 vs BTC Dominance

TOTAL3 (total altcoin market cap excluding BTC and ETH) plotted against BTC dominance. Cowen uses this overlay to size altcoin-season magnitude and identify altcoin-cycle peaks distinct from the BTC cycle.

## Current Thesis

No direct TOTAL3 or BTC dominance chart has appeared in reviewed briefs, but Cowen's structural view continues to explain why altcoin seasons fail to materialize: durable rotation into altcoins requires Bitcoin to reach the 95th percentile or higher on his asymmetric quantile model, a threshold missed in both 2019 and 2025 (topping near the 75th percentile both times). The new brief reinforces this from a different angle — Cowen now frames the 2026 top itself as having occurred "on apathy rather than euphoria," with no altcoin rotation, and points to social interest metrics (~0.2) resembling the low-participation 2018 cycle far more than the euphoric 2022 cycle (~0.5). With his focus shifted to calling a BTC cycle low in the next 60 days (leading candidate: October 2026), and BTC's own recovery deferred to a future cycle, the precondition for TOTAL3 expansion and BTC dominance decline remains unmet, with no near-term mechanism to change that.

## Key Evidence

- In both 2019 and 2025, BTC topped near the 75th percentile on the asymmetric quantile model — well short of the 95th–99th percentile range that historically coincides with full euphoria and sustained altcoin rotation (prior).
- Cowen explicitly links the absence of a durable alt season in 2019 and 2025 to BTC failing to become euphoric: "Bitcoin never became euphoric because it topped out before getting to the 95th percentile." [25:20] (prior).
- Both 2019 and 2025 cycle tops occurred roughly two months before QT ended and the Fed balance sheet began expanding, suggesting monetary policy timing also capped BTC's upper tail (prior).
- Cowen now explicitly attributes the 2026 cycle top to "apathy rather than euphoria," noting no altcoin rotation occurred, and cites social interest metrics (~0.2) tracking closer to low-participation 2018 than euphoric 2022 (~0.5) — direct structural evidence of a suppressed, low-participation cycle rather than a broad alt rotation (6V6rlIV2Zek).
- BTC's rally into resistance in 2026 was much weaker than in 2018 (16% vs ~50% from the June/July low), which Cowen reads as fewer participants and a faster-playing cycle — consistent with no durable TOTAL3/altcoin expansion this cycle (6V6rlIV2Zek).
- Cowen defers BTC's own cycle recovery to the next market cycle, pushing out the precondition for altcoin rotation and TOTAL3 expansion (prior).
