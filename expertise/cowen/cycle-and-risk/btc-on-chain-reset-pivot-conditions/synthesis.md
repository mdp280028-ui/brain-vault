## Current Thesis

Cowen's default framework remains time-based capitulation toward an expected Q4 2026 low, with a price-based reset (a touch of the ~$38K balance price) as the clear override signal, and on-chain indicators like MVRV-Z (0.251) and the risk composite (~0.2) confirmed not yet fully reset. He now layers a macro/cross-asset lens onto this: drawing on 2014, 2018, and 2022 as fractal analogues, he argues Bitcoin's cycle bottom historically arrives only after the stock market's second, later-year correction — not its first — and expects the S&P to extend its midterm-year highs into August/September before a corrective leg (possibly tied to the September 16th Fed meeting) that could bottom as late as Q4. Since 2026 has already seen its "first correction" earlier in the year, he treats the anticipated later S&P correction as the more likely trigger for Bitcoin's true on-chain reset, potentially threatening Bitcoin's ~$60K support if the S&P falls far enough.

- Bitcoin is currently squeezed between the falling bear market resistance band (~$69K) and the rising 200-week moving average (~$63.7K), a convergence Cowen says will force a decisive break within the next couple of months and before year-end (WRftdLFrepU)
