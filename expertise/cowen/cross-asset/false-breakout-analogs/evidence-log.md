# Evidence log: False Breakout Analogs

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-11-12 (QcJlPjcOZjw): Cowen rejects the 2019 and 2023 analogies that bulls used after the break above the previous high. He argues a midterm year makes it look more like a false breakout, as in 2019, 2015, 2020 and March 2022, which turned difficult afterward. He also compares the post-first-rate-hike pattern in March 2022 to now: a rally, fading, then a fall. He says of the higher-low alternative: "This is not my main scenario right now." [08:45]
