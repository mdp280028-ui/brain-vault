Cowen argues the July countertrend rally (possibly extending slightly into August) is following the same seasonal pattern as 2023, 2022, 2018, and 2014 mid-term years, all of which point to Bitcoin turning red again in August and especially September.

## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Cowen says the bond market is "starting to revolt" and the Fed is not raising rates.
2. He frames the current setup as analogous to 2023, 2022, 2018, and 2014, which he says would point to a correction in the latter part of Q3.
3. His prior call was for a low to form by late June/early July — he says this played out.
4. A countertrend rally in July also played out as expected ("check").
5. He allows the rally could extend a bit further into August, but expects Bitcoin to turn back down later in August and especially into September.
6. He cites monthly return patterns for Bitcoin in mid-term years: July tends to be green, but August/September tend to be red (2022: July green, Aug/Sep red; 2018: July green, Aug/Sep red).
7. In 2014, even though July was red, August and September were still red — reinforcing the seasonal red-Q3-tail pattern regardless of July's direction.

## Predictions made
- Cowen expects Bitcoin to come back down later in August and notably in September, consistent with 2023/2022/2018/2014 mid-term-year seasonality.
- Possible modest rally extension into August before the downturn.
