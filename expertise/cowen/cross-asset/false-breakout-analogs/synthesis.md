# False Breakout Analogs

## Current Thesis
Cowen reads Bitcoin's move above the May high (the ~$83K area) as a false breakout, not a durable new leg up. After such breakouts price tends to return to the lows quickly, and he leans on midterm-year analogs (2015, 2018, 2019–2020, March 2022), all of which turned difficult afterward. BTC has now dropped about 12% in a week to ~65K. He sees two paths. One is a high-volume capitulation candle soon, like the 30–50% falls in one to two weeks after past false breakouts. The other, which he calls at least as plausible, is a relief bounce to a lower high within one to two weeks, then another drop before the December FOMC meeting. He expects the final low around mid-December, as in 2018, and says a test of the realized price (~54K) is possible, though he does not claim price is near its floor. He dismisses higher-low and inverted head-and-shoulders talk.
