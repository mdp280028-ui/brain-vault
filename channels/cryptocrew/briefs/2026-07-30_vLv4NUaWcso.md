## TL;DR
Using the monthly Stochastic RSI, he says Bitcoin's momentum dropped below its historic "bloodbath" trigger level in January 2026, starting a ~330-579 day recovery countdown that points to a bottom-to-bull transition between late 2026 and Q1 2027 — but warns each cycle's percentage gain is getting weaker, so million-dollar or even half-million-dollar BTC won't happen next cycle.

## Main points
1. On the monthly BTC/USD Stochastic RSI, every time the orange line has dropped below a specific white threshold level (~17), a bloodbath followed lasting a defined number of days to recover: 335 days (2011), 579 days (2014), ~395 days (2018), and 424 days (2022) — the pattern he says "always" repeats.
2. He traces the current cycle: bearish divergence formed from a swing high in Feb 2024 to a lower high in Jan 2025 to a third consecutive lower high, which broke at $126,000, triggering a price and momentum plummet that dropped the orange line below the threshold level in January 2026, starting the current countdown.
3. Applying the historical recovery range (330 to 579 days) from the January 2026 trigger, he calculates the earliest bottom/turn as end of 2026, the latest as August 2027, and the most likely outcome (~400 days, based on the pattern of the last 8 years) as Q1 2027 — when the bull run would be in its "infancy stages."
4. Central warning: the next bull run will be "unlike anything we've seen before" — he reiterates from a previous video that million-dollar BTC by 2030 is "highly unlikely" and even half-million-dollar BTC won't happen next cycle, because each cycle's percentage gain has shrunk every time (2010-11 gain never repeated, 2011-13 gain smaller and never repeated, 2017 gain smaller still, and the most recent cycle's ~700% bottom-to-top gain will also not be seen again).
5. Longer-term frame: he expects a ~10-year "devastation" period (echoing patterns he says the stock market has shown for 100+ years, roughly every 30 years) within a larger ~40-year cycle, followed by a growth phase similar to but likely smaller than the one just completed.
6. Advises viewers to trade far less, avoid reacting to social-media urgency, and instead focus only on the "bottom golden zone" window (now through end of year) and the "top zone" window for the next couple of years.
