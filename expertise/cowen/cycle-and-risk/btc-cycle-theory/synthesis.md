# BTC Cycle Theory

Cowen's framework that Bitcoin and broader risk assets follow an approximately four-year cycle of bull-top, bear-bottom, and accumulation phases. He frames the cycle as macro-driven rather than halving-driven, citing parallel equity-market cycle precedents going back to the 1950s.

## Current Thesis

BTC remains stuck between the bear market resistance band above and the 200-week moving average below, with each successive test punching slightly further through the range — a tightening squeeze Cowen reads as the market groping toward its next real move rather than as a sign the bottom is already in. His seasonal scaffolding is unchanged: 2026 continues to pattern-match 2018 (now explicitly "a less volatile version of 2018"), with the current July rally read as typical midterm-year seasonal behavior — Cowen quantifies it directly, noting BTC is up ~10% this July versus +20% in 2022 and nearly +40% in 2018, a softer rally than either precedent. He expects this strength to reverse, given that August and September turned red in 2022, 2018, and 2014 alike, and chains this to a specific macro trigger: the Fed holds rates in July but hikes in September as bond vigilantes push long-end yields higher, precipitating the 10-20% stock correction that drags BTC down to its cycle bottom in Q4 — with on-chain and social indicators still only partially reset and a retest of the ~57k February low needed to complete that reset. He retains his fallback: if BTC hasn't broken down by year-end, he'll pivot to declaring the bull market already resumed via time-based capitulation.

## Key Evidence

- July monthly-return comparison: BTC up ~10% this July vs. +20% in July 2022 and nearly +40% in July 2018 — a muted rally by comparison that still fits the seasonal pattern rather than breaking it. (oDvuDHTv2zo)
- In 2022, 2018, and 2014 alike, the July rally was followed by red August and September months, the direct precedent Cowen cites for expecting the current rally to reverse. (oDvuDHTv2zo)
- The Fed-hike-into-correction chain: Cowen expects the Fed to hold in July but raise rates in September as the two-year yield (already above the Fed funds rate) signals policy is no longer restrictive enough, pushing bond vigilantes to drive long-end yields higher and triggering the August-September stock correction that drags BTC to its Q4 cycle bottom. (TOKUXlPBhOs)
- Bitcoin is ping-ponging between the bear market resistance band and the 200-week MA, with each fake-out through either band going slightly further, showing the range tightening ahead of an eventual breakout or breakdown. (JY_wY8XXjYU)
- ROI-from-low day-count: cycle on day 1333 vs. prior bottoms at day 1432/1436 (~100 days out, pointing to October/December); complemented by the ROI-from-peak-to-low chart (day 283 vs. 364/377/406 for prior post-halving-year bears). (oj2n8nkTTTE, sy6AxBbgico, XQN1M-sIuGs)
- Social participation (~0.25) matches July 2018 rather than July 2022 (~0.5); on-chain indicators remain only partially reset, with a retest of the ~57k February low seen as needed to complete the reset.
