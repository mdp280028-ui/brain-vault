## Current Thesis

Midterm years are historically the worst for equities and the "reset window" for Bitcoin, and Cowen says the current Q4 dump is exactly that pattern ("this happens every midterm year"). With BTC at the 21-week EMA (~76.6K) inside the bull market support band (~73–77K), he sees two roughly equally likely endings: a fast FTX-style capitulation that could bottom before the midterms if the band fails, or a roughly one-week bounce at the band followed by a final drop right after the midterms (November/December). This differs from 2014/2018/2022, when BTC held or rose into the midterms, because BTC has now fallen four straight weekly candles going in. He admits he was wrong that BTC would take out the May high, treats the move as a fake-out (two weekly closes below that level would confirm it), and expects a price-based capitulation below the realized price in Q4. His outlook is BTC year ↑2 (hoping for a 2027 rally), months ↓4 and weeks ↓3, with a drawdown smaller than the prior cycle's 77%.
