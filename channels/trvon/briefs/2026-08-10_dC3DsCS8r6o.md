5. On HEX/PulseChain price action: still bullish, calling the recent move a continuation not seen "since back here," and says his bull case holds as long as the pattern stays "pullback, new high, pullback, new high" — he'd flip bearish only if PulseChain rolls over and just keeps falling instead of holding a range.
