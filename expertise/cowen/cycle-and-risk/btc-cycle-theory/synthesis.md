# BTC Cycle Theory

Cowen's framework that Bitcoin and broader risk assets follow an approximately four-year cycle of bull-top, bear-bottom, and accumulation phases. He frames the cycle as macro-driven rather than halving-driven, citing parallel equity-market cycle precedents going back to the 1950s.

## Current Thesis

Cowen now treats the next 60 days (mid-August to mid-October 2026) as the pivotal window for the cycle low, since the historically weak August-October seasonal stretch coincides with where his ROI-from-low and bear-market-length models both point. October remains his lead candidate (~50% odds vs. all other months combined), but he now spells out an alternate path via bear-market-duration comparisons: a 52-54 week bear (matching the last two cycles) points to a low around Oct 5-19, while a 59-week bear (matching the cycle before that) would push the low to the week of November 23 — which he flags coincides with his own ITC conference. He also raises a sideways-chop alternative modeled on 2018, where volatility simply dries up through Aug-Oct rather than dropping sharply, delaying resolution toward November. Structurally he continues to lean on the 2018 analog (same low-February/higher-low-spring/lower-low-summer shape, apathetic rather than euphoric top, social interest ~0.2 resembling 2018 far more than 2022's ~0.5), while stressing on-chain indicators (MVRVZ, PEL multiple, thermocap ratio) still haven't reset to typical bottom thresholds.

## Key Evidence

- Cycle day-count from the low: prior two cycles bottomed on day 1,432 and 1,436; Bitcoin is now on day 1,360, putting day 1,420 (60 days out) within a week or two of where prior cycle lows occurred. (6V6rlIV2Zek)
- Bear-market-duration comparison: last two bear markets ran 52-54 weeks (implying a low ~Oct 5-19); the 2019 (pandemic-shortened) bear ran 37 weeks; the bear before that ran 59 weeks, which would push a low to the week of Nov 23 — the same week as Cowen's ITC conference (Nov 20-22). (6V6rlIV2Zek)
- Seasonality base rates: since 2010, midterm years average a ~10-11% August drop and ~8% September drop, which from current prices (~$63K) implies ~56K, or the low 50s if both hit; seasonality "works maybe about 70% of the time." (6V6rlIV2Zek)
- Four-year-cycle lows have crept progressively earlier each cycle (January → December → November), supporting October as the next logical "candidate month." (6V6rlIV2Zek)
- Structural 2018 analog: same low-in-February, higher-low-in-spring, lower-low-in-summer shape at "10x" scale (60K vs 6K), this cycle's top formed on apathy without altcoin rotation, and social interest (~0.2) tracks 2018 far more closely than 2022 (~0.5); 2026's rally to resistance was much weaker than 2018's (16% vs ~50%), suggesting a faster cycle with fewer participants. (6V6rlIV2Zek, UIv9IQ4uXEA)
- NVRVZ/MVRVZ and broader on-chain risk composite (PEL multiple, transaction fees, market-cap-to-thermocap ratio) have not yet reset to typical bottom thresholds, arguing the low is not yet confirmed despite Cowen's resumed personal DCA-buying. (6V6rlIV2Zek, 96LmewBt0VM, zsnxQO7tC9M)
