## TL;DR
He argues Bitcoin dominance is about to roll over for the first time this cycle, which will unleash the biggest alt/meme season of the run, and lays out a Brett price target (12x to $50M on his $15K position) plus a stablecoin-dominance chart to time the eventual top.

## Main points
1. Bitcoin dominance has risen for 1,000+ days this cycle with no real alt season; he says every meme coin currently held (Brett, Pepe, etc.) has only existed during a rising-BTC-dominance regime and has never seen a true dominance downturn.
2. Even tiny dominance pullbacks produced huge moves: Nov-Dec dominance drop from 61% to 54% sent Brett from $700M to $2.3B market cap; May-June drop sent Brett from $300M to $1.9B — both moves lasted only about a month because dominance kept rising afterward.
3. He's confident dominance is about to roll over because for the first time, Bitcoin price is surging while dominance is NOT rising in tandem, signaling money starting to favor alts/memes; he also cites a bearish technical pattern on BTC dominance — three overshoots of highs with bearish (volume) divergence, a setup he'd normally short.
4. Strategy for playing the dominance shift: pair altcoins against BTC and buy ones showing a long-term double bottom or clear uptrend vs BTC, e.g., Brett/BTC hasn't broken its floor in ~1.5 years despite missile strikes and Trump-coin outflows; buying there limits risk and would already be a 5x just returning to prior pair highs.
5. Key structural point: even when Brett/BTC made a lower high in dominance-pair terms, Brett's dollar market cap top was still higher than the prior cycle's top (1.9B vs 2.3B) because there's more total money in the space now — so lower relative highs can still mean higher absolute prices.
6. Top-timing tool: combined USDT.D + USDC.D dominance (currently ~6% of the $3.74T total crypto market cap) is inversely correlated with BTC/crypto price — chart bottoms mark market tops (March 2024, Dec 2024) and chart tops mark market bottoms (July-Aug 2024, April). He says watch for stablecoin dominance returning to prior support/bounce levels (~4.5% or lower) as the sell signal, since BTC itself has no overhead resistance to reference.
7. Buy discipline: buy alts at a discount vs BTC (floored, unable to break lower) rather than at a premium (post-pump); this is what produces 10x-50x outcomes, and is the opposite of what ~90% of traders do (chasing pumps).
8. He holds fewer than 10 meme coins total (Brett, Pepe, Bobo "and a few others"), arguing that conviction means concentration, not spraying across many bets; says no major new meme narratives are likely going forward except from unpredictable new world events, so he prefers proven coins that have survived multiple deep dips.
9. Brett valuation case: it's the most undervalued #1-meme-per-chain coin (Doge $29B on its chain, SHIB $7.8B on ETH, Solana's top meme $2.1B, vs Brett only $540M on Base) and should "catch up into the billions."
10. Specific Brett math: his $15,000 position is targeted to reach over $50M this cycle — a ~12x from current position value — if Brett goes from ~$500M to ~$6B market cap; using a Pepe ratio framework (last cycle Brett hit ~20% of Pepe's peak market cap), if Pepe hits $40B this cycle, Brett at 20% would be $8B (16x from current), and cites a SHIB-flipped-Doge precedent as reasoning Brett could reach 50-100% of Pepe's cap ($20B-$40B) even if only briefly.
