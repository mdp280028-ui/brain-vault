# Equities & SPX Correlation

SPX (S&P 500) price action, structural corrections, and its correlation with Bitcoin. Cowen treats SPX as the dominant macro asset crypto correlates with during risk-off events, and the four-year cycle as shared across both; SPX cycle-tops and major corrections often coincide with crypto-cycle inflections.

## Current Thesis

Cowen's midterm-year SPX roadmap remains intact: new all-time highs (7750, August 4th) are consistent with prior midterm years, where corrections historically don't begin until mid-August to late-September, often tied to the September Fed meeting (now dated September 16th, ~57% hike / 43% hold odds). He sharpens the historical parallel by noting that 2014 and 2018's corrections both started within days of each other (September 19th and 21st respectively), right around where this cycle's September 16th Fed meeting falls — reinforcing his expectation that the meeting itself could mark the correction's start, in keeping with "the last three midterm years." He stresses that a correction "starting" in that window doesn't mean it "finishes" then — 2014, 2018, and 2022 all bottomed later (mid-October, December, mid-October respectively) — and reframes the SPX/Bitcoin linkage around each cycle's *second, later-year* correction rather than the first: 2026 already had its "first correction" earlier in the year, so the pattern points to Bitcoin's cycle bottom following the coming August/September SPX correction, not preceding it. He maintains rates/yields (10-year at 4.6%, 30-year back to October 2023 levels) as the mechanism pressuring stocks whether the Fed hikes or holds, alongside a hedged downside scenario (SPX retesting toward 6,000, which would likely break Bitcoin's ~60K support).

## Key Evidence

- 2014 and 2018 midterm-year corrections started September 19th and September 21st respectively — near-identical timing that Cowen flags as "interesting" and maps onto the September 16th Fed meeting this cycle. (cmgvWLCes0s)
- Midterm-year correction start dates cluster mid-to-late in the year, not earlier: 2014 (Sept 19, ~10%), 2018 (Sept 21, ~20%), 2022 (Aug 16, ~20%, already a bear market) — supporting new highs persisting into August before any drop. (3yiYSG8sOFE)
- In 2014, 2018, and 2022, Bitcoin's cycle bottom tracked the stock market's *second*, later-year correction, not the first — the basis for expecting BTC's bottom to follow the coming SPX correction rather than the already-completed early-2026 one. (3yiYSG8sOFE)
- The S&P/M2 fractal overlaid from 1996 has correctly mapped both the 2023 ~10% correction and the recent ~10% drop; it points to September 2026 for the larger decline. (prior)
- Rates-side mechanism: two-year yield above Fed funds implies policy isn't sufficiently restrictive; bond vigilantes pushing 10- and 30-year yields higher (30-year back to Oct 2023 levels, 10-year at 4.6%) is named as the proximate trigger for the August-September correction, regardless of whether the Fed hikes or holds September 16th (currently ~57% hike / 43% hold odds). (prior; 3yiYSG8sOFE; cmgvWLCes0s)
- 2018 precedent for BTC downside sensitivity: Bitcoin held $6,000 support most of the year despite the S&P's *first* correction being only 7-8%, and only broke down (~48% drop) once the *second* correction hit — used as the template for why a ~6,000 SPX retest could cost Bitcoin its current ~60K support. (3yiYSG8sOFE)
