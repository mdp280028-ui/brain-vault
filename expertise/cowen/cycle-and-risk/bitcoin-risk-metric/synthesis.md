# Bitcoin Risk Metric

Cowen's proprietary 0-to-1 scaled composite indicator used to identify zones of long-term accumulation (low risk) and distribution (high risk). Buy zones cluster below 0.3 and distribution zones above 0.7; the metric is central to his long-horizon allocation framework.

## Current Thesis

The risk metric anchors a tiered dynamic DCA framework ($100/week at 0.2–0.3, $200 at 0.1–0.2, $300 at 0–0.1), with no buying above 0.3 and no selling until 0.6. The last explicit reading (mid-June 2026) was 0.296, in the buy zone, after sub-0.2 prints earlier that month. The Nov 10 conference preview gives no new metric reading, but Cowen's related on-chain view is that Bitcoin (~73K) has not yet breached realized price (~54K) or balance price (~38K) this bear market, as every prior bear market did. He lays out two paths: a higher low (low 60s) followed by a weaker 2027 rally and a later drop to realized price, or a 2018-style lower low in Q4 that resets on-chain indicators, which he would find more bullish. His timing window for a Q4 low is roughly the next two months (to mid-January 2027); the June expectation of an October 2026 cycle low has slipped into this window.

## Key Evidence

- Last explicit summary risk reading was 0.296, just below the 0.3 buy ceiling; earlier in June it was ~0.18–0.198, a historically rare sub-0.2 zone that marks the third and final bear market stage (hx_neha7BVQ, Di8YR9nX8Q8).
- Bitcoin has historically spent only 14.73% of days in 0.2–0.3, 12.5% in 0.1–0.2, and 2.34% (135 days) below 0.1; dynamic DCA below 0.3 turns $18.3K into ~$2.1M versus ~$700K for equal-weight DCA (hx_neha7BVQ).
- Realized price (~54K) and balance price (~38K) remain unbreached this bear market, which is unusual versus prior cycles and leaves open a further on-chain reset (cpjk4LSO6dg).
- Cowen calls the 87K high an apathetic-top fake-out, with social interest matching 2019, consistent with earlier findings that social risk was lower than at the June 2022 low (cpjk4LSO6dg, hx_neha7BVQ).
- Macro regime explains persistence in accumulation territory: crypto is the furthest thing up the risk curve, and high rates and QT suppress it until a Fed pivot; Cowen now sees the Fed near the end of hiking, with a stock drop in Q4 as in 2014, 2018 and 2022 likely driving the low (5feBKA1-w-Q, cpjk4LSO6dg).
