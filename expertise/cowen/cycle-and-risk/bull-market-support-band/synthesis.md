# Bull Market Support Band

A combined indicator (20-week SMA + 21-week EMA) Cowen treats as the bull/bear regime line for Bitcoin. Sustained closes below it signal bear-market continuation; reclaims signal regime shifts. Also applied to SPX and select altcoins.

## Current Thesis

BTC has rallied back up into the bull market support band (~73K–77K) and is sitting on the 21-week EMA (~76.6K) during what Cowen calls the typical midterm-year Q4 dump, with the 200-day (~73K) just below. He sees two roughly equally likely paths: no support at the band and a fast, FTX-style capitulation that could bottom before the midterms, or a roughly week-long bounce at the band followed by a final drop right after the midterms (a November–December low). He admits he was wrong that BTC would take out the May high, and sees the move above it as a fake-out that two weekly closes below that level would confirm. He expects BTC to eventually trade below the realized price, but with a smaller drawdown than last cycle's 77%, and hopes for a 2027 rally. For gold, the analogous monthly band (20-month MA/21-month EMA) remains structural bull-market support, with a bottom expected in the June–October midterm-year window (carried from prior briefs; not addressed in this video).

## Key Evidence

- BTC is at about 77K on the 21-week EMA (76,612), with the low printed at about 76,642. The band spans roughly 73K–77K and the 200-day is near 73K (g2MQiby7_qY)
- Cowen says there are two roughly equally likely outcomes. One is a break through the band with no support and a fast capitulation before the midterms. The other is a bounce at the band for about a week, then a final drop after the midterms (g2MQiby7_qY)
- Prior midterm-year lows (2014, 2018, 2022) came right after the midterms. This time differs because BTC fell for four weekly candles heading into them. Cycle-day counts (prior bottoms on days 1432 and 1436, now day 1439) point to a bottom window within days (g2MQiby7_qY)
- Equities are confirming weakness (Google, Amazon and Intel earnings drops, Nasdaq down about 9%), but the bear market has had no volume-spike capitulation. Cowen says to watch for a price-based capitulation below the realized price, which has happened in every midterm-year Q4 (g2MQiby7_qY)
- Earlier in the cycle, BTC was squeezed between the falling bear market resistance band (~$69K) and the rising 200-week MA (~$63.7K). It first closed below the 200-week MA in late June 2026, mirroring 2022 and 2018 (WRftdLFrepU)
- Gold's monthly band (20-month SMA/21-month EMA) has historically been retested, and briefly broken, during bull markets without ending them. Gold's ~30% correction is comparable to prior non-terminal drawdowns (mo9PUWbEJ-4)
