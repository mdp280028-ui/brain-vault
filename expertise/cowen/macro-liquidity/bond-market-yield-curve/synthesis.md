## Current Thesis
Cowen and fellow panelists argue the bond market, not the labor market, is the key macro risk: the 30-year yield is breaking to new highs and the 10-year is approaching its October 2023 level, echoing a 2023 setup where a July-October yield breakout coincided with a ~10% S&P drop. The two-year yield trading above the Fed funds rate signals policy is no longer restrictive enough, and new signals from Fed's Kevin Walsh (no forward guidance, a 3-of-12 dissent favoring an immediate hike, and a rigid 2% target) reinforce the case for a September rate hike. The group now expects this to drive a back-half-of-2026 stock correction alongside a Bitcoin cycle bottom, most likely in Q4.
