# Business Cycle Phase

The position of the US economy within its expansion-peak-contraction-trough business cycle, including Cowen's M2-normalized composite (SPX, unemployment, rates, inflation, M2). Cowen overlays the business cycle with Bitcoin's four-year cycle to time long-horizon allocation, treating cycle-bottoms as accumulation windows.

## Current Thesis

Cowen continues to run two overlapping cyclical lenses — the four-year Bitcoin cycle (a "less volatile" 2018 fractal replay) and the business-cycle/liquidity lens (resembling the 2019-2020 "digestion phase" after the 2019 apathetic top) — with "nothing really changes this year" as his summary framing. He now emphasizes that Bitcoin/crypto sits well below his fair-value regression trend line, near the lowest percent-deviation reading ever recorded outside 2009-2010, and is "not opposed" to further downside, expecting weakness to persist for at least a few more months and possible fresh lows before year-end as the four-year cycle plays out. He has stepped back from precise near-term date-calling (no fresh month/week-specific bottom call this brief, versus the base-cased late-September/early-October cycle-bottom estimate in prior briefs) in favor of a broader "weak for months, lower before year-end, then a fresh bull market" stance, while continuing to frame industry-level "growing pains" (scams, rug pulls, security breaches, declining retail interest) as a symptom of the late-cycle/digestion phase rather than a separate concern.

## Key Evidence

- Cowen frames the current period through two simultaneously valid lenses: the four-year-cycle fractal (2026 as a less-volatile 2018 replay) and the business-cycle/liquidity lens (resembling the 2019-2020 digestion phase after the 2019 apathetic top), now summarized as "nothing really changes this year" (2o0qSlwjkv8).
- The asset class trades well below Cowen's fair-value logarithmic regression trend line, with the percent deviation near the lowest ever recorded except for 2009-2010 — "the asset class is still coming in well below the quote-unquote fair value logarithmic regression trend line" [01:35] — and he is "not opposed to this thing going even lower" [02:43], expecting weakness for "at least a few more months" [04:26] and possibly a fresh low before year-end (2o0qSlwjkv8).
- BTC is squeezed between the 200-week moving average and the bear-market resistance band; Cowen has previously expected one more retest/rejection at the resistance band to "fully reset all the onchain indicators" ahead of the true cycle bottom (Wf7vUIoGQrE [09:22]).
- Base case for the market-cycle bottom had been late September/early October 2026 via low-to-low ROI measurement, with a December tail case (2013-2014 analog); this brief does not repeat a specific date, instead reiterating only that weakness continues "for now" before a fresh bull market (Wf7vUIoGQrE; 2o0qSlwjkv8).
- BTC's ~$57.7K early-summer low is a near-exact 10x match to 2018's $5,743 low, reinforcing the four-year-cycle analog underpinning the fractal-based timing framework (n9yTkx3r904, 8TmOvGK235I).
- Inflation has been above the 2% target every month since 2021, and under Warsh's Fed, ~84.5% market-implied odds exist of at least one hike by December 2026 — a late-cycle macro headwind Cowen treats as backdrop rather than the primary timing signal (prior).
