## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Cowen frames Bitcoin's four-year cycle as still intact: the cycle governs when lows occur (approximately every four years), not when highs occur, and comparisons to the S&P 500's own four-year low pattern (2018, 2022) show the phenomenon isn't unique to crypto.
2. Measuring from the cycle low, Bitcoin topped on day ~1,050-1,059 this cycle, nearly identical to the prior two cycles (day 1,069 and day 1,059) — supporting the "topped when it always tops" thesis.
3. Measuring bottoms from the low, the last two cycles bottomed around day 1,432-1,436; Bitcoin is currently on day 1,354 of a cycle that historically runs 1,430-1,440 days, meaning a bottom is close but probably not yet in.
4. Measuring from the all-time-high peak, this bear market has been milder than the prior three (likely because there was no euphoric top/retail rotation into alts), and Bitcoin is currently on day 304 versus an average bottom around day 406 — roughly two-thirds through on this measure.
5. Measuring from the halving, Bitcoin is behaving similarly to prior post-halving periods where price trended down or stagnated for an extended period without a clear breakout.
6. Cowen's base case bottom window is late September through mid-December 2026, based on prior cycle bottoms occurring in January (three cycles ago), December (2018), and November (last cycle) — questioning "why not October."
7. Bitcoin's 2026 year-to-date ROI resembles a less volatile version of 2018, and sits within one standard deviation of the average of the last three midterm years.
8. Cowen draws a macro analogy to 2019: Bitcoin topped about two months before QT ended in both 2019 and 2025, both years had three Fed rate cuts, no rotation into altcoins, and a top driven by apathy (falling social interest, rising BTC dominance) rather than euphoria — he sees the 2026 bear market aligning with the 2019 post-top downtrend.
9. Key difference from 2019: back then Bitcoin arguably bottomed via price-based capitulation, whereas this cycle looks more like time-based capitulation, which historically takes about a year to play out (bear market is already ~10 months in).
10. On moving averages, Bitcoin has followed the same 50-week → 100-week → 200-week breakdown sequence as the last three bear markets, currently sitting around the 200-week MA, reinforcing "this time is not different."
11. Cowen says he personally started DCA-buying Bitcoin again after it dropped below $60K, framing his own strategy as buying through the back end of the bear market despite the risk of near-term further downside.

## Predictions made
- Bitcoin bear market bottom expected in the window of late September through mid-December 2026, with October flagged as a plausible specific month.
- Bitcoin is on day 1,354 of a historical 1,430-1,440 day cycle-low pattern, implying the low is not yet in but is approaching within roughly 76-86 days from the time of recording.
- Cowen expects the bear market to stay weak for "a few more months" from the point of recording.
