# Evidence log: Government Shutdown Btc Analog

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-11-19 (ixMSi_KX89s): Cowen flagged that another government shutdown is possible on December 12. Using the average of the last seven shutdowns, BTC sold off, levelled out, and sold off again into them, then rose afterward. He compared this to 2018, when BTC fell, bounced, and sold off into the 4-year cycle low. The brief has no direct quote on the shutdown analog specifically.
