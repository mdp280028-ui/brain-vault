## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Gold is trading around $4,000/oz, below that level, and down about 5-6% year-to-date as measured from the yearly open.
2. Cowen reiterates the pre-2026 thesis that gold would likely see a sizeable midterm-year correction, and it's currently down about 30% from its high.
3. Historical bull-market drawdowns for context: 2022 saw a ~22% drop, 2008 saw a ~34% drop before bottoming, and 2006 saw a ~26% drop.
4. Cowen says he cares more about timing the low than predicting the exact price level, since the drawdown magnitude is harder to call than the seasonal window.
5. Comparing year-to-date ROI in 2026 to 2018 and 2022 midterm years, gold lines up closely with both prior cycles; 2022's midterm-year low bottomed in late September, and 2018's bottomed in mid-August, averaging to an August-September bottom window.
6. Base case: gold's low forms sometime between July and October, with an outside chance of July given that Bitcoin historically bottoms in early July across midterm years on average.
7. The gold-specific bull market support band (distinct from the Bitcoin one) is around $3,800, roughly a 4% drop from current price; gold last tagged this band in 2023, and briefly broke below it in 2022 and 2008.
8. After the midterm-year low forms, Cowen expects the gold bull market to potentially continue into the end of the decade, with price expanding to a new higher high.
9. Silver is now at its own bull market support band and has underperformed gold since Cowen's February call that the low gold-silver ratio favored gold over silver.
10. Silver could draw more attention once gold's bottom is confirmed; a break below $50/oz would represent a "deep value zone," though Cowen thinks it's premature to call that now, drawing an analogy to the 1970s where silver bounced along support for a year or two before resuming its uptrend.

## Predictions made
- Gold's midterm-year low likely forms between July and October 2026, with the bull market support band near $3,800 (~4% below current price) as the target zone.
- After the low forms, Cowen expects the gold bull market to potentially continue into the end of the decade, aiming for a new higher high.
- Bitcoin, on average across prior midterm years, bottoms out in early July.
