## TL;DR
Markets are nervous and money is rotating out of chips and AI into consumer stocks. Kevin sides with Morgan Stanley over Goldman: he is bullish on hyperscalers, Broadcom and Microsoft, and expects the Anthropic IPO to open above $100 tomorrow. He calls the consumer rotation a bubble.

## Main points
1. Anthropic IPO (ticker ANT) finished pricing for underwriters that day, with trading starting tomorrow. He guesses it opens over $100, with pricing near $85, because price ranges are set low to drum up hype. Later headlines he read: about $500B of orders (about $400B institutional, near $100B retail), and an October run rate of $81B. He says each outage helps Anthropic. He frames it as another "sucketing" that could recycle money back into chips.
2. Export rules and Jensen: He reads the interim final rule as a headquartered-in-the-US exemption, so US hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Oracle, SpaceX, maybe Meta) could legally rent compute to China, even from Southeast Asia, with BIS licenses. He calls it "smuggling to legally renting" and says it could raise sales. Microsoft is nearing a $500M Bytedance deal, which is a "drop in the bucket" against about $400B of revenue but sets a precedent. He values Microsoft at about a 1.0 PEG, "dirt cheap" (EPS about $20.03 mid-2027, 19% growth).
3. Nvidia and Jensen: Nvidia's sales team introduced Alibaba to Alice Hong's Megaspeed, and Kevin thinks it was legal at the time. Hearings: Jensen is expected to testify Dec 1 (House Foreign Affairs, private) and Dec 3 (public, Senate Banking, per CNBC), with Tim Teter on Dec 2. The Commerce committee also wants interim CEO Colette Kress to testify. Nvidia is still down despite the earnings beat. Kevin keeps a 40% belief that Jensen returns, versus Kalshi at 10%, and holds a small long.
4. Iran: He says the US has a blockade plan (naval blockade, sanctions, Operation Iron Gate). Iran is lashing out at ships, Bahrain and Kuwait, and the US struck Qeshm Island. He thinks Trump has no motive for a deal after the election, and calls it "slapping the flea". Oil still doesn't care: Brent is down over $26 in 3 weeks to under $92, supply is above prewar levels, and the diesel crack spread is $40, back to April levels, versus a usual $10-20.
5. Fed: Waller wants more aggressive hikes, floating 50bp, with inflation at about 3.5% annualized. Futures price 3.1 hikes through September, with 100% for December and 73% for January. 5-year breakevens are at a low. His read is that the Fed is hawkish, inflation expectations are falling, and financial conditions are tightening.
6. Bank of America survey: 3% see a recession. 52% expect an AI capex cut in the next 6 months, up from 30% in October and 15% in September. Semis positioning fell to 39% long from 45% and 55%. Hedge funds are at a -3 z-score on US semis. Only 36% say international stocks win, 24% US, 17% gold. He stays with the US and sees gold doing poorly under a Warsh Fed. He calls the credit event fear dated: Oracle and CoreWeave CDS are stable off their October highs but not falling.
7. Morgan Stanley (Wilson): 65% of the Russell 3000 is down more than 20% since June. Capex growth goes from 62% in 2027 to 14% in 2028. S&P target is $8,700 base and $10,000 bull within 12 months (S&P at $7,300), at 20x $500 of 2028 EPS. The capitulation indicator is near Lehman levels, "very close to a major low", though still possibly some work to do if the Fed stays hawkish. Nasdaq 100 valuation is at a "100 year low". Oil is seen at $75 by Q4 2027 and core inflation at 2.5%. The call is to buy AI adopters and hyperscalers, and Kevin agrees.
8. Goldman versus Kevin: Money is going into consumer discretionary and staples (Target +5-6%, Take-Two +8%, Cheesecake +4%, Wynn +2%, Disney +2.7%). He prefers decade-long holds. Valuations: Disney is at a 1.45 PEG with upside to about $165. Cheesecake is at a 2 PEG and about 35% overvalued. Broadcom at 355 is a 0.67 PEG with 67% to 2-3x upside, and it was rejected at 355 again. He says Bank of America warns that staples are in a bubble, and he agrees.
9. Outages and cyber: The recent run of outages (Microsoft 365, FAA, Chase, NSA, Apple) is officially blamed on bugs and maintenance. He is skeptical. AI is finding 39x more bugs (Chrome 433 vs 11), Microsoft patched 1,400 flaws and Oracle 4.7x more. Mandiant puts the time-to-exploit at negative 7 days, and the FBI is warning about the open-source hacking tool Rex. Costco panic buying (21 of 26 generators sold out) drives the bump. Generac is at about a 1 PEG. Cyber stocks are interesting: NET +5%, CRWD +6%, S +8%.
10. Memory stocks: A lot of damage is priced in, though he is not saying it's the bottom. SanDisk's EPS is expected to shrink toward 2031, but at 2031 EPS of $71 it trades at 10x, and Micron is at a 0.3 PEG. He prefers owning a 30-50 stock basket and not going all-in on one.
11. Close: Nasdaq (QQQ) went green, up about 30bp, while Nvidia stayed red. AMD failed a breakout at 520. Robinhood is at his $69 entry target but he hasn't entered. Promotion for the Black Friday sale and the Reinvest terminal is skipped.
