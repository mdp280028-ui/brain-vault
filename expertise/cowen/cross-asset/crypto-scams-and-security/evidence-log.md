# Evidence log: Crypto Scams And Security

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-08-02 (2o0qSlwjkv8): Cowen attributes part of the crypto industry's current weakness to scams, meme coin rug pulls, "presidential rug pulls," and recent hacks of cold storage devices causing user losses. He ties declining YouTube views across crypto channels to retail newcomers losing money to scams and leaving before researching legitimate projects, and criticizes commentators who frame altcoin rug pulls as "good for Bitcoin," calling that view badly mistaken and self-interested. He frames the period as an industry-wide "maturation process" that should eventually reward legitimate builders over scammers.
