# Fed Policy & Rates

The Federal Reserve's policy-rate decisions, FOMC meeting cycles, rate-cut and rate-hike paths, and the politics around Fed leadership transitions (Powell, Warsh, successors). Cowen treats Fed policy as the primary macro driver for crypto and equities.

## Current Thesis

Heading into the September 16th FOMC, Cowen has shifted his odds meaningfully toward a hold: market pricing now shows only ~44% odds of a rate hike (down from 57% in the prior brief), driven by a badly missed non-farm payroll print (23,000 vs. ~80,000 expected) even as unemployment itself keeps falling (4.1%, down from 4.5% in November) on shrinking labor-force participation rather than job growth. Despite the softer labor data, he stresses layoffs and initial claims remain at pre-recession, even 50-year lows, so this reads as labor-market weirdness rather than recession — his recession dashboard sits at a low 0.16 reading. His core seasonality thesis is unchanged and in fact reinforced: he still expects a stock correction to start by late September at the latest, tied to the September 16th FOMC and consistent with prior midterm-year patterns (2014, 2018, 2022), which continues to underpin his Q4 2026 Bitcoin cycle-bottom framework. Conviction on equities has firmed slightly (SPX now ↓3 months, up from ↓2), while BTC's long-term outlook stays modestly positive (↑2 years).

## Key Evidence

- Market-implied odds of a September rate hike fell to 43.9%, down from Cowen's prior 57%/43% coin-flip framing, following a non-farm payroll miss (23,000 actual vs. ~80,000 expected) (Ju6Q-D9GZZU)
- The 2-year yield remains above the Fed funds rate, which Cowen notes has historically (e.g., the 1990s) preceded the Fed "chasing" the 2-year yield down rather than holding firm (Ju6Q-D9GZZU)
- Initial jobless claims recently printed ~189,000, the lowest in roughly 50 years, and layoffs are back to pre-pandemic levels — used to argue labor weakness is not yet recessionary despite the payroll miss (Ju6Q-D9GZZU)
- Cowen's ITC Macro Recession Risk Dashboard reads only 0.16, with recession risk low for 4-5 years, though he expects the business cycle to end "within the next couple of years" (Ju6Q-D9GZZU)
- The 2014 and 2018 midterm-year corrections both began within days of a Fed meeting — September 19th and September 21st respectively — which Cowen continues to flag as direct precedent for the September 16th FOMC this cycle (cmgvWLCes0s, Ju6Q-D9GZZU)
- 10-year yield at 4.6% and rising, 30-year already back to October 2023 levels — cited previously as pressure on risk assets even in a "hold" scenario (3yiYSG8sOFE, prior)
- Three of twelve FOMC members dissented in favor of an immediate hike at the July meeting, and Warsh has abandoned forward guidance ("play the ball and not the referee") (q2QLLihl6cc, prior)
