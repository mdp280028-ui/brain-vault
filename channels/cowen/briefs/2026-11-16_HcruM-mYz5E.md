## TL;DR
Cowen says Bitcoin (~65K) is in a window of weakness after a false breakout and a ~12% weekly drop. He thinks we are close to the bear-market low in time, though not necessarily in price, with a final low likely around mid-December near the FOMC meeting. The realized price (~54K) is a level to keep in mind.

## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Bitcoin is around $65K after falling more than 12% this week, and almost 17% from the weekly open. After the false breakout above the highs (the $83K area), he says price tends to return to the lows quickly.
2. A further 17% drop would take BTC to the realized price, around $54K. He does not claim the price floor is near, but thinks the bear market is close to ending in time.
3. He sees sentiment turning: people who were optimistic all year are now pessimistic. He says this is what you see near the end of bear markets.
4. He dismisses the "inverted head and shoulders" and higher-low talk, saying such patterns fail more often than they work. He admits the size of the summer rally surprised him. He had expected 75–80K but not 87K.
5. Through the lens of rate hikes, BTC is retracing the path after past September hikes in midterm years: a hold, then a fall in November–December. He sees the next month or two as a window for a new low. If no lower low by about mid-January, one becomes less likely in the short term.
6. Scenario one: BTC does not bounce, and a high-volume capitulation candle ("final candle of surrender") arrives soon. He compares this to false-breakout capitulations that fell 30–50% in one to two weeks (2015, 2019–2020, March).
7. Scenario two, which he calls at least as plausible: any relief rally gives a lower high in about one to two weeks, then another drop before the December FOMC meeting.
8. He cites the 2018 analogy, where the final bottom came in mid-December around the FOMC meeting. He flags the FOMC meeting (Dec 9) and a possible government shutdown around Dec 12 as mid-December events to watch. He also notes that recent highs have tended to fall on the 21st of the month and local lows mid-month.
9. He says an OpenAI or Anthropic IPO next year could be a catalyst for a future BTC drop, not necessarily to a new low, and another reason for a rotation out of Bitcoin.
10. He warns that people who spent the year denying the realized-price risk will likely not admit their mistake if it happens. He says bear markets make fools of both bulls and bears.

## Predictions made
- Next month or two is a window where a new low is possible, with a lower low unlikely if none by about mid-January.
- If BTC gets a relief bounce, a lower high comes in about one to two weeks, followed by another drop before the December FOMC meeting.
- The final low likely comes around mid-December, near the FOMC meeting, as in 2018.
- OpenAI IPO, likely next year, is a potential catalyst for a future BTC drop.
