## Current Thesis

Cowen continues to frame 2026 as tracking the 2018 fractal almost exactly: February low, higher low in spring, a swept low in late June/early July, and a rally into the bear-market resistance band by late July/early August. With BTC now rangebound (63.7K-67K) and up ~10% this July versus +20% in 2022 and +40% in 2018, he expects rejection near resistance followed by a pullback into August-September, with Q4 remaining the historically likely window for a full on-chain reset and possible final drop/cycle bottom — though his conviction on the longer-term downside call has softened (year-ahead outlook cut from strongly bearish to more tentative) and he now concedes the cycle low could already be in, just capped by lingering fear-driven selling rather than a fresh leg down. He continues to attribute the market's "different this time" feel to this cycle topping on apathy (~126K) rather than euphoria, not to any actual structural break from prior cycles, and notes retail participation remains conspicuously absent versus the 2017-style blow-off.

## Key Evidence

- The 2018 analog maps onto 2026 in sequence and price behavior: February low → higher low in March/April → June/July capitulation sweep → rally to the bear market resistance band by late July/early August — a pattern Cowen says has held "every month this year." (lfYYC7qxDQ8; prior rWfLRMiyQVY)
- Historical July returns quantify the seasonal edge: 2026 is up ~10% this July, versus +20% in July 2022 and ~40% in July 2018, with all three of 2018, 2022, and 2014 showing red August/September following July strength. (lfYYC7qxDQ8; prior n9yTkx3r904)
- Historically Bitcoin hasn't durably broken above the bear market resistance band until well into the post-halving year (January, March, or June/October in prior cycles), implying any strong sustained upside remains far off even if the current rally continues. (lfYYC7qxDQ8)
- In both 2018 and 2022, BTC's July-rally-then-giveback pattern was essentially identical, but the reversal's timing and ETH's behavior diverged (2018: sooner, new ETH lows; 2022: later, ETH higher low) — meaning the BTC seasonal pattern alone doesn't settle which unwind path 2026 follows. (prior; ygVIJCGLLSQ)
- On-chain indicators haven't fully reset for a cycle bottom (MVRV Z-score not below zero; realized/balance price still above prior-cycle bottom levels), implying more downside is plausible before a true low, consistent with an expected August-to-Q4 resolution. (prior; sigSZCnSa6M)
- Cowen flags seasonality is not universal — 2022 crashed in May instead of rallying, 2014 fell in July instead of rallying — as an explicit caveat on how much weight to put on the pattern. (prior; n9yTkx3r904)
