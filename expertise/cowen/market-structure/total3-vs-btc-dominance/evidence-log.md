# Evidence log: TOTAL3 vs BTC Dominance

Append-only. Newest entries at bottom.

- 2026-05-07 (PnveYgxtd1w): This brief does not directly analyze TOTAL3 or BTC dominance; its relevance to the topic is indirect. Cowen frames BTC as broadly undervalued relative to traditional assets and expects it to "stay undervalued for the rest of the year" [00:22], deferring recovery to the next market cycle. Because a sustained TOTAL3 rally and BTC dominance decline historically require BTC to first complete its own cycle strength phase, this cycle-deferral view implies the TOTAL3 vs BTC dominance overlay has not yet generated an actionable altcoin-season setup.

- 2026-05-30 (uFn3KUE-VTI): Cowen's new asymmetric tail curvature quantile model provides a structural explanation for the absent altcoin seasons of 2019 and 2025: in both cycles BTC topped near the 75th percentile, never reaching the 95th–99th percentile range associated with full euphoria and sustained rotation into altcoins. He states directly: "Bitcoin never became euphoric because it topped out before getting to the 95th percentile." [25:20]. With BTC currently at the 9.4th percentile and midterm downside scenarios pointing to $48–58K, an upper-tail breakout — the precondition for TOTAL3 expansion — remains structurally off the table for this cycle.

He also notes 2026's rally into resistance was much weaker than 2018's (16% vs ~50% from the June/July low), reading it as evidence of fewer participants and a faster-playing cycle — both points reinforce why TOTAL3 has not expanded and BTC dominance has not broken down this cycle, though no direct TOTAL3/dominance chart appears in this brief.
