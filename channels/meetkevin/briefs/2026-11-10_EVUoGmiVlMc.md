## TL;DR
Hot October CPI (core +0.3% m/m for a third straight month, headline +0.4%) makes a December Fed hike a near-certainty. Kevin says a 50bp hike is now on the table and that the Qs must regain 675 or sell off.

## Main points
1. Pre-CPI: Waller said the Fed could do 50bp on a hot CPI and 25bp on a softer one, and wants to "take back the three insurance cuts." Hike odds for December were 99.8%, and 2.43 hikes were priced by Sep 2027, the same as before the election. Kevin says the comment was unhelpful because it "killed the odds."
2. The print: headline +0.4% and core +0.3% (0.296 unrounded), both hotter than the expected +0.3% and +0.2%. Shelter rose 0.3% and made up 30% of the monthly increase. Kevin calls the report "broad-based": motor vehicle insurance +0.7, airfare +4, recreation +0.9, medical care +0.3, and water and sewer up. Coffee fell 3.6%.
3. Trend: citing Nick T, core is the third straight 0.3% month, and the 3-month annualized rate is 3.75%, up from 1.6% in January. Kevin says that is roughly double July's pace. Super core is 3.52% year over year and +0.4% m/m, slightly below the expected 0.423. He says it is stuck at a support level and is not breaking down.
4. Rates reaction: the 2-year yield rose 14bp and the 10-year rose ~7-10bp to about 4.84-4.94%. Traders priced ~28bp of hikes for December, and the Dec hike odds read 114%. September 2027 hikes rose to 2.63. The debate has moved from whether to hike to how much, and the curve should flatten until the Fed stops hiking.
5. Iran: no Iran deal means high fuel prices (Waller said diesel won't come down soon). Oil is falling today, which Kevin reads as insiders expecting a deal. A deal before the December meeting is the only window in which the Fed could skip a hike.
6. Labor: ADP weekly was 19.75K versus 16.5K expected, which he calls a stable to strengthening labor market. Barkin says risks are on the inflation side and spending is stronger than expected.
7. Markets: a guest says higher rates and slower earnings growth are a poor setup for big gains next year, with political uncertainty hitting the AI trade. Kevin says hyperscalers are rate sensitive too, the Nasdaq is falling, and rate-sensitive stocks will "suck today." The 5-year inflation expectation is only slightly higher.
