## Key points (the distilled video)
1. Cowen reiterates his base case that gold's low will occur sometime between now and the July to October window.
2. He compares 2026's year-to-date ROI of gold to 2022, noting gold is at exactly the same point in the cycle now as it was then.
3. He notes it feels worse this time because gold came from more elevated levels, but the relative positioning is the same.
4. In 2023, 2024, and 2025 gold trended back up, but 2026 (the midterm year) has been weak, consistent with the seasonal pattern.
5. In the 2022 midterm year, gold bottomed in late September; in 2018, gold bottomed in mid-August.
6. 2026's year-to-date ROI is lined up closely with both the 2018 and 2022 trajectories.
7. Averaging out 2018 and 2022, gold is currently right at the average trajectory of the last two midterm years.
8. On average, those two prior midterm years bottomed in the August to September timeframe.
