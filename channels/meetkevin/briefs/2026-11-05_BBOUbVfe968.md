## TL;DR
Kevin calls the Nasdaq 100's roughly 5-6% crash a capitulation, driven by Democratic-sweep fears about data centers plus leveraged selling. He argues AI spending is accelerating, not ending, and says to buy the dip in semis and real estate.

## Main points
1. Open: the Qs hit 666 and then roughly 650, down 4-6% on the day, with Nvidia down ~8%, AMD down 13% and Meta down 7%. Only Applovin (+30%), Eli Lilly, Disney and Six Flags were green at points. QQQ rejected the 700 floor yesterday.
2. Momentum: Morgan Stanley's tech momentum factor is down 62% from highs, worse than the dot-com low of -55%, and that chart predates today. Risk appetite is below the "Leopold" level but not as low as Liberation Day or the yen carry trade. The SOX had its worst move since Leopold and Liberation, and is down 30% from highs. RSP is near all-time highs.
3. Fed: falling stocks tighten financial conditions. He argues the Fed is unlikely to hike into a bear market. December hike odds are ~70%, and hikes priced by Sep 2027 fell from ~2.4 to 1.98. Kalshi odds on exactly one hike this year rose from 21% to 33%, and he holds that bet.
4. The cause is a "perfect storm" (whiteboard): data-center delays, then CDS up, then liquidity down, then blue-wave moratoria fears, then leveraged stocks crushed. Semis are the most hyped sector and the most sensitive to liquidity and the yield curve. Real estate is the least hyped. Excess liquidity is near 2022 lows.
5. Microsoft: it did not cut spending. Q2 capex is over $50B, ~$170B for the calendar year, and he says capex accelerates in the second half. The delays at Nscale and Iren come from cabling spec changes, missing cooling gear and permits. Off-balance-sheet AI commitments rose from $1.8T in June to $3.1T in August to $4.11T. Amazon's commitments went from ~$125B to $254B. More than 250 US data-center projects are delayed.
6. CDS: Oracle and Meta CDS are down from peak and CoreWeave is flat at peak. SpaceX CDS topped out Oct 30. So the "doom loop" is not worsening. Meta priced its 30-year debt about 90bp wider than its April bonds. Apple raised $7B, its first bond sale in over a year.
7. Politics: Democrats won governorships in Texas, Ohio and Georgia. 41% of planned data centers are in states with existing or proposed moratoria. New governors take office in January, so he expects legislation around March-April. He calls the fears mostly overblown. He also says Vance blamed the Iran war and Trump agreed, which removes the pressure for an Iran deal. Oil is up ~3%, and he says a deal would bail out stocks, yields and oil.
8. Labor and productivity: Q3 productivity rose 3.6% annualized versus 2.1%. Unit labor costs fell 0.7%, which he reads as weaker worker pricing power. Jobless claims are at a 1969 low, though he calls them lagging. ADP was 86K. His two recession canaries are labor and AI spend, and both are still fine. The risk is a crash forcing layoffs.
9. Valuations: Micron PEG 0.35, Microsoft ~0.5, Lumentum 0.72, Marvell 0.68, Broadcom 0.58, Nvidia 0.59, SanDisk 1.2, Applovin ~1. Goldman says the market prices a 65% cut to 2027 memory earnings, with potential for the sector to double. The memory semiconductor index is at its lowest ever. He is bullish on NAND and says to buy the dip. A guest cites Nvidia at 14x forward earnings. Nvidia reports Nov 17.
10. Liquidations: Walleye Capital ($17B AUM) fired its head of equities and is returning capital after losing 5.9% in October. He cites rumors of other pod blowups, 3x leveraged ETF losses (one down 85%) and a retail seller who liquidated his account. Barclays warns correlation could spread the selling. QQQ has had 13 trading sessions down 5% intraday, all in the dot-com collapse.
11. Positioning: he recommends a barbell of semis and real estate. He is ~90% real estate, buys long-term holds, and says to avoid leverage. He cites midterm seasonality as needing ~50 days of digestion and says volatility could last 6-12 months. CNN Fear & Greed is at extreme fear.
