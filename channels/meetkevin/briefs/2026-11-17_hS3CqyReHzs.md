## TL;DR
Live Nvidia Q3 FY27 earnings stream. Kevin called a beat-and-raise and got a bigger one: $115.8B revenue and a $130B guide. He calls it a bullish report and Nvidia dirt cheap, and says the Anthropic IPO is the next catalyst. He also reads the call as closing the door on Jensen's return.

## Main points
1. Pre-earnings setup: the market sat at the 650 "pain point" (QQQ), tested twice that day and also on Nov 5. He blamed hedge fund selling (per Goldman), Chinese chip export bans, the Jensen resignation and the SEC probe. Street expectations were EPS 2.47 and revenue $109.33B, with Nvidia beating in 7 of 8 quarters. He guessed $111B revenue and a guide of about $120B.
2. Catalyst framing: before Jensen the drivers were the election, the Fed, the Iran deal and CPI. Now there are two new ones, the Nvidia forecast and Anthropic's IPO performance. His 800 QQQ target is "delayed", so he calls for patience and cash security, preferring sold calls or puts and no debt.
3. Credit and rates context: CoreWeave and Oracle 5-year CDS are off their peaks but still elevated, which he reads as credit risk being somewhat overblown. Markets price 3.13 Fed hikes by the end of next year, which he calls "not ideal". He also cites Morgan Stanley's Mike Wilson arguing the S&P is forming a bottom and that hyperscalers and AI winners are undervalued, and says he agrees.
4. SEC probe: a WSJ report landed just before the close. Kevin thinks the SEC cares about disclosure, not whether an investment is good. He checked the 10-K risk factors and found language on export-control violations, and he thinks the probe is mostly political cover for Trump.
5. The print: revenue $115.8B was a 5.9% beat on $109.33B. The guide is $130B plus or minus 3% (midpoint, versus his 120 guess), and the 3% band is wider than the usual 2%. There is a $150B buyback expansion, and purchase commitments rose to $390B from $279B (+39.7%). Gross margin was 74.9%, operating cash flow $48B and free cash flow about $65B. Vera Rubin added nearly $20B, edge computing was up 42%, and licensed China Hopper sales were $2.4B with none in the guide.
6. Circularity check: equity purchases held at about $15B, while debt-securities purchases fell 48% (to $11.4B from about $21B). He also flags the OpenAI guarantee of $105B, which could expand to about $200B. Pricing power is shown by revenue up 2.03x against cost of revenue up about 92-95%, and opex up only 56%.
7. Call takeaways: FY28 growth is raised to about 75% from 70% (Kevin notes she misspoke "75 to 75"). Frontier labs are 14-15% of data center revenue, which ties Nvidia to the Anthropic IPO. Cloud backlog is $2.5T. Gross margin bottoms in Q4 at 71-72%, then 72-73% in FY28. Management says it will be more rigorous on guarantees and will return excess free cash flow. They said they have no intention to reinstate Jensen. She downplayed a post-midterm slowdown, and Kevin backs that with Cisco CEO comments that data center builds will go ahead.
8. Valuation: after hours about $160 versus $9.33 EPS gives a 17 P/E. With average growth of 33.15% over four years that is a PEG of 0.51, which he calls dirt cheap. If growth were cut to a third, it would still be worth about 76% more. His argument is that the slowdown is already priced in. GPU rental prices are rising (H100 about $2 to $3, B200/B300 about $4.50 to $7), and he blames hedge fund degrossing for the chip selloff.
9. Jensen and CEO odds: Kevin is "disappointed" and sees a real shot the board is moving on. On Kalshi Jensen fell from 9% to 8% while Colette rose to 8% and then 9%. Earlier Jensen was 13%. Kevin holds a small long on Jensen's return.
10. Other stock views: he is bullish on Anthropic's IPO (expected to price tomorrow and trade Thursday) and thinks it will do better than SpaceX. He sees AMD, Broadcom, Intel and Dell as buyable again, SanDisk and Micron buyable near $800 for SanDisk, Super Micro as cheap, Robinhood at his $69 target, and OpenDoor as an avoid. He prefers SpaceX but says Tesla is the better buy if the merger goes through. Cyber stocks should rise on outages but fell, and Salesforce was down 9%.
11. Wrap-up: the stock was up about 3.5% after hours, just under 164. He calls it "a bullish earnings report" and says patience and diversification are needed. He took the dip on a basket of about 30 stocks, and said he is not buying only Nvidia. Course and Kalshi promotion is skipped.
